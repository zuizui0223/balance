# BALANCE

`balance` is the Chapter-2 theory and evidence-synthesis repository for persistence inside a conflict-active middle world.

The core theoretical object is a **sandwiched regime** in which a shared biological coordinate is under genuine functional conflict (`L > 0`) while the optimized shared architecture still outperforms the best currently accessible differentiated alternative (`W_S* > W_D*`). Under the decomposition used to connect the sister SCH and BITA theories, this is equivalent to `Phi = sL-K < 0` when the direct and decomposed worldline definitions are concordant.

## Current paper spine

The manuscript is organized as:

```text
theory mechanism
→ reality-pattern recovery
→ compatible quantitative lanes
→ boundary / negative-control structure
→ remaining direct-identification gap
→ focal worldline validation last
```

The literature synthesis does **not** estimate natural prevalence and does **not** directly identify BALANCE occupancy. The current source-adjudicated pattern ledger contains 17 independent biological clusters, including 9 middle-regime signatures. A strict Q1B lane contains three independent effect-size-ready positive clusters plus design-matched negative controls and explicit boundary cases.

### Important Q1B interpretation

Positive Q1B admission requires the preregistered same-coordinate conflict pattern. Therefore the three-cluster random-effects result is a **conditional positive-case summary of magnitude and heterogeneity**, not an unbiased design-wide meta-analytic mean and not an independent statistical test of recurrence. Recurrence is supported by recovery of the registered positive pattern in three independent biological systems; matched negative controls show that comparable designs do not inevitably produce it.

The current conditional Q1B summary uses:

- `Fragaria vesca`
- `Impatiens capensis`
- `Gymnadenia conopsea` (2015)

All four modified Knapp–Hartung 95% intervals include zero at `k=3`, and two components show moderate heterogeneity. Moderator analysis is preregistered but prohibited at `k=3–4`; a first exploratory moderator fit requires `k>=5` and at least two independent clusters in each represented moderator level.

## Evidence layers

The empirical package keeps incompatible objects separate:

```text
STRICT_POSITIVE
  three effect-size-ready Q1B clusters

NEGATIVE_CONTROL
  Trifolium repens
  Lythrum salicaria

BOUNDARY
  Ipomopsis sequential-filter case
  Primula farinosa discrete-morph case

ATTRIBUTION_FAIL
  Gymnadenia 2019 residual nonpollinator case

BROAD PATTERN LEDGER
  17 independent clusters
  9 middle-regime signatures

DIRECT WORLDLINE RESERVE
  0 matched shared-vs-differentiated receipts
```

The final direct empirical target is a same-context, common-fitness-scale comparison of optimized shared and differentiated-accessible architectures with joint uncertainty. Until that exists, `W_S*`, `W_D*`, `rho`, `Phi`, `xi`, and `d_B` remain outside the literature-based empirical claim set.

## Claim ceiling

Appropriate current claim:

> A finite conflict-active persistence regime exists theoretically. Independent literature systems recover recurring conflict-compatible and middle-regime persistence signatures, while matched negative and boundary cases delimit the mechanism. The quantitative Q1B pool describes effect magnitude and heterogeneity conditional on positive admission. Existing literature still lacks the matched shared-versus-differentiated worldlines required for direct BALANCE occupancy.

Not appropriate:

> The positive-selected Q1B pool estimates the universal effect of conflict, the source ledger estimates natural prevalence, or the literature synthesis directly identifies `rho`, `Phi`, `W_S*`, or `W_D*`.
