# Timing × biological function pair × architecture: empirical common-support audit

Date: 2026-10-20. **Status: actual source-screen descriptive audit; not representative macro inference, not a fitted coefficient, not an experimental fitness-worldline comparison, not a historical structural origin.**

## First scientific result

In the already-screened BALANCE data, a source's annotation **SIMULTANEOUS** is **not an experimentally identified joint functional optimum** and cannot supply the PAYOFF model's same-coordinate simultaneous conflict floor `C=ab*Delta²/(a+b)`. Nevertheless it helps locate which ecological source systems have even potentially relevant temporal geometries.

This audit independently reads the current source-frozen U2 and PLANT_PILOT screens, and separately checks U3's matched extraction against its frozen case-control dependence map. It does not open BALANCE V4 independent-confirmation outcomes and **never pools U2, the pilot and U3 as independent species**, since both source selection and taxon reuse differ.

## 1. U2: conflict-positive sexual interference frequently remains shared

Source: [U2 original-review screen](../data/BALANCE_PLANT_U2_SCREENING_V1.csv), **22** screened species, **8** conflict-positive. Among the eight positive original-source records:

| Published timing geometry code | Positive records | Structural-state characterization |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `MIXED` | **5** | Three `SHARED_INTEGRATED`, one `TEMPORAL_SEPARATION`, one `TEMPORAL_AND_SPATIAL_SEPARATION` |
| `SEQUENTIAL_WITHIN_UNIT` | **2** | One `SPATIAL_SEPARATION`, one `POLYMORPHIC_OR_MOSAIC` (structural yes/no unresolved) |
| `SIMULTANEOUS` | **1** | One `SHARED_INTEGRATED` |
| **Total** | **8** | No source-confirmed structural module division; one source structural status unresolved |

The single simultaneous/positive source record is a **qualitatively tagged source case**, not an identified `do(z)` pure-function optima contrast, and the eight cases are selected through a sexual interference review rather than a botanical denominator.

## 2. PLANT_PILOT: temporal coding and anatomical resolution are confounded by functional pair

Source: [BALANCE selected plant pilot](../data/BALANCE_PLANT_MACRO_PILOT_V1.csv), **29 source-context records** (28 dependency groups), **16** coded `POSITIVE` for measured/tested conflict.

Among the **eight** positive records with source tag `SIMULTANEOUS`:

| Function-pair family | Source rows | Observed routing architecture |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `POLLINATOR_ANTAGONIST` | **4** | Four `SHARED_INTEGRATED` |
| `POLLEN_REWARD_GAMETE` | **3** | Three `WITHIN_FLOWER_DIVISION_OF_LABOUR` |
| `POLLINATOR_PREY` | **1** | One `SPATIAL_SEPARATION` |

Across **all sixteen** positive pilot rows, timing codes are `SIMULTANEOUS` **8**, `SEQUENTIAL_WITHIN_UNIT` **3**, `MIXED` **2**, `CONTEXT_DEPENDENT` **2**, `SEASONALLY_ALTERNATING` **1**. Seven pollinator–antagonist positive records retain `SHARED_INTEGRATED`, but three pollen-reward/gamete-positive records all show within-flower division. Other sexual-interference cases employ temporal, spatial, mosaic or among-flower routes.

**New formal result: zero complete within-function-pair 2×2 support tables** for source timing tag (simultaneous/other) × structural yes/no among positive pilot source records. For every recorded functional family, at least one of the four cells is empty or missing. Therefore the timing tag's **conditional association with structural division** is not fully nonparametrically identified in this selected pilot, let alone its causal effect. Naively fitting `structural ~ simultaneous + function_family` or reporting the 3/8 ratio as a population evolution parameter would be unsupported.

The apparent contrast (four concurrent pollinator–antagonist cases still shared versus three concurrent pollen reward/gamete cases with division) is a **source-grounded comparison deserving study**, not independent evidence for a general effect: source inclusion was partly based on familiar specialized morphologies, and geometry/functional pair/study design can be aliased.

## 3. U3 independent route-negative controls expose morphological proxies

Source: [U3 original matched source extraction](../data/BALANCE_PLANT_U3_MATCHED_EXTRACTION_V1.csv) and [frozen taxonomic dependence map](../data/BALANCE_PLANT_U3_DEPENDENCE_V1.csv): **four PASS pairs**, but only **three** taxonomic source-dependence blocks (Solanum, Melastomateae, Senna).

- Four cases recruited for visible heteranthery: **4/4** have source-positive pollen reward/gamete conflict and within-flower functional division. This cannot estimate natural conflict prevalence.
- Three of four nonheterantherous controls are also conflict-positive, with *Solanum lycocarpum* showing **among-flower** division, *Senna spectabilis* **within-flower** division, and *Senna covesii* having **unresolved broader routing**. *Osbeckia chinensis* remains **conflict- and routing-unresolved**, not a verified conflict-free zero.
- Morphological heteranthery and functional routing are **not bijective**: `Senna alata` and `S. spectabilis` have different visible fertile-stamen morphological classes yet within-flower pollen routing; `S. lycocarpum` and `S. spectabilis` share absence of visible heteranthery but differ in the level of function allocation.

The U3 observations are drawn from an independent selection process and are not an additional 8 independent replicates of the pilot. Cross-universe taxon reuse and within-Senna comparative dependence are not waived.

## 4. What is *not measured* by these source screens

The following must not be inferred from `conflict_timing_geometry`, `architecture_mode` or `module_substrate`:

| The new SLK three-strategy question | Status in U2/PILOT/U3 original-screen fields | Legitimate measurement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simultaneous opposing pure-function fitness gradients on one trait `z` | `NOT_IDENTIFIED` | Matched `z × isolated function × relevant environment` interventions, independently supported optimum scales |
| Plastic and reversible within-genotype reaction norms `W_P^*` | `NOT_CODED/NOT_IDENTIFIED`; **not biologically absent** | Mark/clone the same individual/genotype, repeated environmental states, reciprocal reversibility and costs |
| Functional overlap duration and time-resolved `C` | `SOURCE_TIMING_TAG_ONLY`, not a measured loss or simultaneous time-exposure strength | Within-flower time-aligned pollen export, visitation/grooming and viable gamete delivery |
| Cue reliability and environmental autocorrelation | `NOT_IDENTIFIED` | Repeated environmental state/cue time series; do not substitute flower-sex expression transition frequency |
| Differential net architecture value or cost `K` | `NOT_IDENTIFIED` | Optimized matched `W_S^*,W_P^*,W_D^*` plus separately assayed maintenance/production or bounded pre-cost worlds |
| A heritable origin of new modules | `NOT_IDENTIFIED` | Nuclear developmental genetics, ancestry, repeated independent changes, exclusion of exaptation, plasticity, drift |

This is directly compatible with the new [SLK three-strategy G1–G9 handoff](https://github.com/zuizui0223/slk/blob/integration/plasticity-functional-routing-gates-20261020/docs/THREE_STRATEGY_VALUE_AND_ORIGIN_GATE_V1.md), the [PAYOFF cue-memory robustness](https://github.com/zuizui0223/payoff/blob/theory/plastic-compromise-to-modules-20261020/theory/TWO_CUE_MEMORY_AND_IRREDUCIBLE_CONFLICT.md), and [BITA's mechanism/evolution causal synthesis](https://github.com/zuizui0223/bita/blob/integration/tradeoff-architecture-causality-20261019/docs/TRADEOFF_TO_EVOLVED_ARCHITECTURE_CAUSAL_SYNTHESIS_V1.md).

In particular, `TEMPORAL_SEPARATION` does **not** imply phenotypically reversible `PLASTIC`; `WITHIN_FLOWER_DIVISION_OF_LABOUR` does **not** imply an independently evolved nuclear module; an observed morphological component interaction does **not** identify a causal architecture construction cost.

## 5. Specific next data collection/extraction, in the proper order

1. **Outcome-independent taxon × function-pair frame:** select taxa based on flora/phylogeny/ecology rather than known heteranthery status, and record source/study effort plus missing records. Maintain independent U2, pilot and U3 universes separately.
2. **Time-resolved functional overlap:** for plants whose stamens act as pollen reward and export machinery, record time-stamped pollinator manipulation, pollen removal/export and pollen availability during the same visit (or consistent flower-level time window). Do not code `C` from two fates alone.
3. **Within-genotype reversibility:** expose marked plants to crossed conditions repeatedly and measure whether the **same physical trait** returns to its earlier state, rather than using across-species architectural differences as a proxy.
4. **Matched multi-option fitness worldlines:** estimate `W_S,W_P,W_D` at an identical reproductive-fitness time horizon and include pollen-siring + maternal viable seed success, geometry and costs; this is a prospectively required new experiment, not a retrospectively identified value.
5. **History after contemporary payoff:** link population genotype, nuclear phylogeny and ancestral trait states, condition on environment and compare alternatives. Confident morphology ancestries alone cannot establish trade-off-caused origin.
