# Sensitivity to unknown fate within the combined fruit category

Source: the authenticated Richman receipts from run 45390074548 and run 45384106598, as archived at 9acc1a1ef77439bae0ba6ec68b47c8036875523c. Let s be the mean plant-level share recorded as successful/developing, a be recorded abort, and l be the combined dehisced/dropped/fly-damaged share. Counts close s+a+l=1 for the 119 positive-denominator plants. Define a hypothetical resolved-success share s+q*l, q between 0 and 1. q is an unknown share-weighted success fraction, not a measured probability or an assumed biological fate. This definition also assumes all recorded developing fruits count as successes. The treatment-control difference is linear in q_t,q_c; evaluate the six vertices of the feasible polygon |q_t-q_c|<=h. Report common-q (h=0) and independent (h=1) as well as intermediate sensitivity. No statistical coverage is implied by the ranges.

For the matched positive-denominator subset (C/P/S 37/33/31), pollen means are taken from the prior original-source variant alternative_metadata_fraction, which uses the same pollen availability and positive total_fruit+aborts denominator; the sole zero-total-fruit plant also has zero aborts by count closure. The common-q stage contrast is ln[(s_t+q*l_t)/(s_c+q*l_c)] minus observed plant-mean pollen lnRR. Its zero is solved algebraically. It is a hypothetical outcome-definition sensitivity, not a newly observed effect, a causal mechanism or a new independent study. No imputation, no same-flower inference, no V4 modifications.
