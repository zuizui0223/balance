## Scrophulariaceae provenance repair

The previous canonical representative, `Verbascum phoeniceum`, was supported by a 2017 heteranthery paper. That was acceptable as explicitly post-review evidence, but it was unnecessary.

Manning & Brothers (1986) directly describe `Diascia anastrepta` as markedly heterantherous. They distinguish a strongly reduced anticous anther pair producing little or no yellow pollen from a posticous pair associated with the stigma and producing copious grey-green pollen. The species itself was described by Hilliard & Burtt in 1983 and remains accepted in Scrophulariaceae.

`Diascia anastrepta` therefore replaces `V. phoeniceum` as the canonical independent Scrophulariaceae representative.
