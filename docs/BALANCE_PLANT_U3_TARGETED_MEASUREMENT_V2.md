# BALANCE plant U3 targeted measurement contract v2

## Purpose

Version 2 supersedes v1 as the current prospective measurement contract.

The public-evidence ceiling is already frozen. The remaining tasks are therefore **measurement problems**, not invitations to keep weakening literature criteria. V2 adds the missing `Senna covesii` routing contract before any new qualifying routing result is observed.

## Monochoria controls

The v1 rules are retained unchanged.

For both `M. australasica` and `M. cyanea`, eligibility closes only through either:

1. a single-visit transfer route with conspecific stigma deposition attributable to the visit; or
2. an animal-access / exclusion or single-visit experiment with a fruit or seed consequence.

Territorial behaviour, genus-level buzz-pollination statements, morphology, or a visit without transfer/reproductive effect remain insufficient.

## Osbeckia chinensis

The v1 rules are retained unchanged.

The binary pollen-fate conflict gate closes only through:

1. same-visitor reward-removal plus export/deposition measurement; or
2. a pollen-removal manipulation linked to export, stigma deposition, fruit set or seed set.

Pollen extraction alone and stigma contact alone remain insufficient.

## Senna covesii — routing architecture

`S. covesii` already has positive binary pollen-reward versus gamete-transfer conflict. The unresolved quantity is **where that conflict is routed**.

Morphology is deliberately not allowed to answer this question. The seven fertile stamens are weakly differentiated / near-homomorphic, but `S. spectabilis` has already shown that equal fertile-stamen morphology can coexist with strong positional within-flower routing.

### Positional groups frozen before outcomes

The assay uses the pre-outcome homologous positions:

```text
median group   = four median fertile stamen positions
abaxial group  = three abaxial fertile stamen positions
```

Those groups may not be redrawn after seeing pollen placement.

### Route A — position-resolved source tracking

Source-label pollen from the two frozen positional groups and quantify, after standardized sonication or a controlled single visit:

- pollen placement across predefined bee body sectors;
- grooming / collection fate;
- stigma deposition or export.

A reproducible non-exchangeability of those fates can identify `WITHIN_FLOWER_DIVISION_OF_LABOUR`.

### Route B — position-selective manipulation

Randomize flower-level shielding, occlusion or removal of median versus abaxial positions, while controlling total pollen availability or explicitly modelling the induced quantity difference.

Measure visitor-body placement and stigma deposition / export after a standardized sonication or single visit.

This route identifies routing only if the positional manipulation changes the registered pollen-fate outcomes beyond a mere pollen-quantity effect.

### Route C — shared-routing equivalence

A claim of `SHARED_INTEGRATED` requires stronger evidence than “no significant difference.”

Before the positional outcomes are inspected:

1. freeze an equivalence margin justified by assay repeatability or an external biological threshold;
2. source-resolve pollen from both positional groups;
3. require confidence intervals for the registered routing contrasts to lie inside that margin;
4. require no independent positional, petal-mediated or among-flower route on the same registered surface.

Therefore:

```text
P > 0.05
!=
SHARED_INTEGRATED
```

## Explicitly forbidden shortcuts

For `S. covesii`, none of the following closes routing:

- seven fertile stamens plus monosymmetry;
- genus-level feeding/pollinating-stamen homology;
- Irwin–Barneby remnant two-set morphology by itself;
- buzz visitation or pollen reward alone;
- pooled pollen deposition without source assignment;
- analogy from `S. spectabilis`;
- a non-significant positional contrast without equivalence testing.

## Why this matters

The programme has already established that binary conflict presence is nonidentifying: conflict persists in nonheterantherous controls.

The next biological object is **routing conditional on conflict**. V2 prevents the strongest remaining control from being assigned to a desired architecture merely because its stamens look uniform.

## Claim ceiling

This is a prospective measurement contract. It does not assign `S. covesii` to an architecture, close any current gate, estimate a routing effect, or identify historical causation.
