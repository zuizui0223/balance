# BALANCE prior-art boundary v1

## Purpose

This document prevents Chapter 2 from claiming novelty for general results that already exist in specialization, pleiotropy, modularity, and generalist-specialist theory.

## Closest prior theory

### Rüffler, Hermisson & Wagner 2012

> Evolution of functional specialization and division of labor. PNAS 109:E326-E335. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1110521109.

This work already develops a broad theory of when initially undifferentiated modules become functionally specialized. Trade-off geometry, performance curvature, synergistic interactions, robustness, and other features can favour or oppose specialization.

**BALANCE must not claim:**

```text
trade-offs can favour specialization;
generalized function can remain favoured;
robustness can oppose specialization;
extra costs would make specialization harder.
```

### Guillaume & Otto 2012

> Gene Functional Trade-Offs and the Evolution of Pleiotropy. Genetics 192:1389-1409. DOI: 10.1534/genetics.112.143214.

This work already asks whether multifunctional genes remain pleiotropic or specialize, and shows that the answer depends on functional trade-offs, fitness mapping, interactions, robustness, and redundancy.

**BALANCE must not claim:**

```text
partial functional sharing can persist;
complete subfunctionalization requires restricted conditions;
multifunctionality versus specialization is itself a new theoretical question.
```

### Clune, Mouret & Lipson 2013

> The evolutionary origins of modularity. Proceedings B 280:20122863. DOI: 10.1098/rspb.2012.2863.

This work already shows that explicit connection costs can favour modular network organization.

**BALANCE must not claim:**

```text
modularity has costs;
costs of maintaining or building connections can shape modularity;
adding a cost term to an architecture comparison is by itself novel.
```

### Generalist-specialist and historical-contingency theory

Long-standing adaptive-landscape and generalist-specialist models already show that costs, selection regime, multiple peaks, and initial conditions can generate compromise strategies or history-dependent outcomes.

**BALANCE must not claim:**

```text
compromise phenotypes exist;
history dependence or multiple attraction basins are generically new;
switching costs can in principle generate hysteresis.
```

## Defensible BALANCE contribution

The candidate contribution is narrower and operational.

BALANCE links an **independently identified one-coordinate ecological conflict** to an **independently optimized alternative-dimensional worldline** and treats the interior between those two empirical statements as an estimand-bearing ecological domain.

The registered bridge is:

```text
SCH receipt
L > 0

BALANCE direct worldline receipt
Delta_W = W_D* - W_S*

BALANCE identified when
L > 0 and Delta_W < 0

BITA decomposition when available
Delta_W ?= sL-K.
```

This yields quantities that are not the generic statement that generalism persists:

```text
xi                two-sided middle-world position
rho               reserve to the differentiated boundary
d_B               minimum two-sided boundary margin
W_e               environmental width of middle-world occupancy
N_B               number of connected BALANCE domains
delta_parallel    direct-vs-decomposed bridge residual
```

and tests that are explicitly cross-chapter / cross-worldline:

```text
direct versus decomposed critical-point concordance;
same versus separated environmental crossings;
re-entry relative to registered monotonicity conditions;
functional-state versus structural-architecture middle-world promotion;
bounded matched-context receipts on one fitness scale.
```

## What is potentially new versus merely useful notation

### Potentially substantive

1. **Direct middle-world identification before mechanism decomposition.** Chapter 2 can be tested from `L>0` plus a matched direct `W_D*-W_S*<0` without first estimating `s` or `K`.
2. **Two-sided empirical localization.** `xi` and `d_B` locate an observed context relative to independently registered SCH-facing and BITA-facing boundaries.
3. **Cross-worldline reconciliation.** `delta_parallel = Delta_W-(sL-K)` forces the direct architecture comparison and the decomposition to agree on context, scale, and cost definition rather than silently treating them as interchangeable.
4. **Critical-concordance programme.** Direct and decomposed environmental zero crossings are independently estimated and classified as common, separated, multiple, or unresolved.
5. **Claim-level separation.** Functional-state middle-world evidence is not promoted to structural-architecture evidence without a structural-y and architecture-cost gate.

### Useful but not sufficient for novelty alone

```text
0 < L < K/s;
q = sL/K;
rho = K-sL;
switching-cost hysteresis;
existence of generalist / compromise regions.
```

These can support the framework but should not carry the novelty claim by themselves.

## Submission novelty sentence

A safe provisional sentence is:

> The contribution is not a new claim that costs can preserve multifunctional or weakly specialized phenotypes. It is an operational framework for measuring the ecological domain between an independently identified shared-trait conflict and an independently evaluated differentiated worldline, and for testing whether direct and decomposed estimates locate the same architecture boundary.

## Claim ceiling

Until empirical Gate V2 is closed in at least one biological system, describe BALANCE as:

```text
a validated theoretical and analytical middle-world framework with observational critical-region anchors
```

and not as:

```text
a demonstrated universal biological phase;
a new general theory of specialization;
a causal explanation for historical modularization;
an empirically established architecture middle world.
```
