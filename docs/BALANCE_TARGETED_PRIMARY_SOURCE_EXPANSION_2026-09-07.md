## Experimental hysteresis: cross-domain dynamical analogue

Faassen et al. 2015, *Oikos*, DOI `10.1111/oik.02006`, experimentally increased and then decreased light stress in a cyanobacterial population and found a delayed, path-dependent recovery response consistent with hysteresis between alternative population states.

Registered class:

```text
HYSTERESIS_OR_PATH_DEPENDENCE
```

Strict ceiling:

```text
CROSS_DOMAIN_DYNAMICAL_ANALOGUE
NOT_TRAIT_ARCHITECTURE_BALANCE_EVIDENCE
```

Its role is only to show that forward/reverse threshold asymmetry is biologically realizable at population scale. It does not show that a shared multifunctional architecture persists against a differentiated alternative.
