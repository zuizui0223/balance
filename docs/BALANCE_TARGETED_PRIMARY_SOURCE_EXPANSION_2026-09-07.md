## Cross-domain hysteresis analogue

Faassen et al. 2015, *Oikos*, DOI `10.1111/oik.02006`, experimentally increased and then decreased light stress in a cyanobacterial population and found delayed, path-dependent recovery consistent with hysteresis between alternative population states.

Registered class:

```text
HYSTERESIS_OR_PATH_DEPENDENCE
```

but with a strict ceiling:

```text
CROSS_DOMAIN_DYNAMICAL_ANALOGUE
NOT_TRAIT_ARCHITECTURE_BALANCE_EVIDENCE
```

Its role is to establish that the forward/reverse threshold logic used by BALANCE is biologically realizable at population scale. It does not show persistence of a shared multifunctional trait architecture against a differentiated alternative.
