# BALANCE U3 unresolved representative candidate audit v1

## Purpose

The three remaining U3 representative identities are not filled by plausible names.

```text
Malvaceae
Bixaceae
Scrophulariaceae
```

Wiley exposes the exact Supporting Table S1 filename, `NPH_3430_sm_TableS1.doc`, but every registered direct-download route returns HTTP 403. The dedicated recovery workflow therefore records a genuine access failure rather than silently treating a search snippet as the supplement.

This audit narrows the independent-source search and freezes false-positive guards.

## Malvaceae

`Sparrmannia africana` is a biologically tempting candidate because it is a buzz/bee-associated Malvaceae with conspicuous stamen differentiation.

However, the accessible morphology describes yellow outer structures as sterile and the inner stamens as fertile. That creates a definition conflict with a strict two-pollen-fate heteranthery reading. More importantly, no exact Table S1 linkage or pre-2010 species-specific source tying this taxon to the Vallejo-Marín family representative has been recovered.

Status: **OPEN, not promotable**.

## Bixaceae

`Cochlospermum vitifolium` is the strongest current discovery candidate.

Pre-2010 experiments establish:

- Bixaceae/Cochlospermum identity;
- pollen as the only floral reward;
- poricidal anthers;
- buzz pollination;
- quantitative pollen removal and deposition;
- outcrossing reproductive biology.

Morphological descriptions also indicate unequal stamen positions/lengths.

What remains missing is the decisive link: a source-secure species-level demonstration that those stamen subsets are the two functionally differentiated heterantherous types used by the 2010 review, or an exact Table S1 row.

Status: **OPEN**.

## Scrophulariaceae

This is especially vulnerable to historical-family-name leakage.

`Agalinis` and `Pedicularis` occur in older Scrophulariaceae literature, but APG III places them in Orobanchaceae. A modern direct Agalinis study also reports that its dimorphic stamens do not show the division of labour typical of heteranthery.

`Torenia` has later heteranthery evidence but belongs to Linderniaceae under the relevant modern circumscription and the evidence is post-2010.

These taxa are retained as explicit rejection receipts so an old broad-family label cannot be mistaken for the review's APG-III Scrophulariaceae row.

Status: **no admissible candidate yet**.

## Selection contract

A representative may be promoted only with either:

1. exact Supporting Table S1 linkage; or
2. species-level pre-2010 heteranthery evidence, secure APG-III family placement, and enough uniqueness to identify the review representative independently.

Generic didynamy, buzz pollination, poricidal anthers, or conspicuous fertile/sterile stamen differentiation are not sufficient by themselves.

## Current consequence

The U3 family universe remains 16/16 source-closed at the **family** level, while representative identity remains 13/16 resolved.

That distinction is intentional. The unresolved three do not block the already registered six species-level matched cases, but they do block any claim that all review representatives have been reconstructed.
