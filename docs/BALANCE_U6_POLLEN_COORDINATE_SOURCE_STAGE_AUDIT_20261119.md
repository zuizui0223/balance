# BALANCE U6 — source-side pollen-stage evidence audit (2026-11-19)

## Purpose and inference wall

**Status: post-open, exploratory audit of existing original evidence — NOT a re-adjudication or a new ecological experiment.** The original 21 U6 taxa, 21 included source reference IDs, 22 admitted source×taxon records, 63 SCREENED predictor receipts, 42 A/B architecture slots and registered V4 design remain **completely unchanged**.

The frozen U6 source-screen compares two proposed fates of one source-side pollen pool: gamete export/transfer versus consumed or inefficiently removed pollen. This audit asks whether the *specific stage* measured by primary sources actually matches that conceptual coordinate, and whether an admission source contains any evidence of a plant reproductive-fitness endpoint.

Do not mistake `DIRECT_REMOVAL_FITNESS_COST` (original Pass-1 admission label) for confirmed causal quantified loss in a matched fitness experiment. Do not count all 21 as independent studies demonstrating realized architectural conflict.

Of the four reproductive-output-labelled systems, the recorded group IDs are **Clusia arrudae**, **Campanula americana**, **Grevillea barklyana**, **Melastoma affine**. They remain heterogeneous in experimental design and fitness endpoint. The tag is not permission to pool effects, nor proof of a shared-versus-differentiated structural fitness worldline.

There are **22 included taxon-reference records** yet only **21 different primary reference IDs** and **21 unique groups**. One reference ID `U6_REF_078` (Kirk 1987) covers *Actinidia deliciosa* and *Echium plantagineum*; both species share a research programme. Conversely, *Theobroma cacao* has two distinct included primary references (`U6_REF_153`, `U6_REF_155`) but one species group. Equal raw counts of reference IDs and groups do **not** imply biological independence.

## Three fixed source-stage flags

These are **post-open review questions** informed by original published descriptions, *not* updated Pass-1 acceptance decisions or filled independent coder A/B records.

| Taxon | Exact original mechanism | Mismatch/limit relevant to the frozen source-coordinate model |
|---|---|---|
| *Melastoma affine* | Honeybees remove pollen **already deposited on recipient stigmas**, reduce fruit and seed set when last visitors | Reproductive fitness effect is real in the original source, but this is **post-deposition stigma removal**, not consumption from the donor anther pollen pool |
| *Zea mays* | Predatory ladybirds eat wind-dispersed maize pollen during anthesis; field airborne pollen deposition and insect gut contents were measured | **Post-dispersal pollen food** is not a demonstrated loss from source pollen available for export or maize male mating success |
| *Nothofagus pumilio* | Parakeets destructively harvest wind-pollinated flowers and empty pollen from them | **Whole-flower destruction bundled with pollen consumption**; no isolated pollen-specific siring/seed fitness effect |

Original studies:
- Gross & Mackay 1998, *Biological Conservation* (doi: [10.1016/S0006-3207(98)00010-X](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0006-3207(98)00010-X)). The original report explicitly distinguishes stigma removal from anther collection.
- Lundgren, Razzak & Wiedenmann 2004, *Environmental Entomology* (doi: [10.1603/0046-225X-33.4.958](https://doi.org/10.1603/0046-225X-33.4.958)). Original measurements address insect predator consumption of windborne corn pollen and natural-enemy dynamics.
- Díaz & Kitzberger 2006, *Austral Ecology* (doi: [10.1111/j.1442-9993.2006.01637.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1442-9993.2006.01637.x)). Parakeet food ecology includes direct destructive flower harvesting, rather than an isolated floral pollen-cost treatment.

The first two are **hard source-coordinate review flags** for a hypothetical strict source-pool sensitivity. The third is a **bundled-mechanism flag**, not an assertion that flowers consumed by parrots have no effect on pollen transfer. None is automatically excluded.

## Exact counterfactual *screening* sensitivity, **not** a refitted model

The following are **hypothetical source-side exclusions** only. The U2 eight positive screened groups remain fixed. The registered frozen 21 U6 groups are still the primary denominator after this audit.

| Scenario | Hypothetical U6 groups | U6 SIMULTANEOUS / ORDERED | U2+U6 groups | V4 predictor rank |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| All frozen source-screened groups | 21 | 18 / 3 | 29 | 5/5 |
| Remove two stage-coordinate flags | 19 | 16 / 3 | 27 | 5/5 |
| Remove those two plus bundled flower destruction | 18 | 15 / 3 | 26 | 5/5 |
| Keep only the four original **fitness-tagged** U6 groups | 4 | 3 / 1 | 12 | 5/5 |
| Fitness-tagged excluding the stage-incompatible *Melastoma* group | **3** | **2 / 1** | **11** | **5/5** |

Every row has full *predictor-only* rank and passes pooled >=2-per-level module/timing support because the unchanged U2 groups carry additional variation. **But source-side U6 ORDERED support falls to one in the fitness-tag-only sensitivities** — so within-U6 timing replication fails that minimum support requirement. Strict cross-universe G1 remains unreachable under frozen V1 because U2 SINGLE+SIMULTANEOUS is only one; it cannot be rescued by removing U6 rows.

**Interpretation:** A rank-full matrix may rely on sources that measured different parts of the pollen pathway, and a broad pollen-theft universe can be much larger than the literature reporting downstream plant fitness. This is a positive outcome of source-evidence accounting, not a positive architecture-selection effect.

## Scientific next steps

1. Ask a source-only independent reviewer whether *Melastoma* and *Zea* identify the same donor-pollen pool as the frozen U6 hypothesis; keep the original Pass-1 admissions and predictor receipts unchanged until formal evidence-versioning.
2. Do not infer temporal routing adaptation just from 3 U6 sequential visitor-order cases. The original chronology is **two theft-before-transfer cases and one transfer-before-later-theft**. Visitor timing is not experimentally demonstrated evolved anther dosing.
3. For any stronger future source-stage causal analysis, separately record donor anther export, pollen on pollinator bodies, deposition on recipient stigma, loss after deposition, and offspring/siring fitness. Measure source cohort/treatment identity, rather than linking stages simply because a paper reports them.
4. Wait for genuine blinded A/B architecture classification and independent predictor adjudication before fitting V4. This exploratory source-stage audit must not be supplied as completed architecture outcomes or used to cherry-pick a corrected denominator.
