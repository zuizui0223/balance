# BALANCE plant U2 strict conflict screen v1

## Purpose

Apply the same strict conflict gate used for the outcome-blind U1 herbivory-pollination universe to the Barrett (2002) sexual-interference review universe.

The question is deliberately narrow:

> does the primary evidence identify a fitness cost or performance conflict between male and female function, rather than merely describing a floral polymorphism or self-incompatibility?

## Result

Across 22 review-defined dependency groups:

```text
POSITIVE                    8
NO_DEMONSTRATED_CONFLICT    3
UNRESOLVED_CANDIDATE       11
```

Decisions:

```text
PASS_CONFLICT_GATE       8
FAIL_CONFLICT_GATE       3
HOLD_FOR_FULL_TEXT      11
```

This is sharply different from the provisional U1 first-20 screen, where no system passed the strict conflict gate.

The two review universes have different inclusion criteria, so this is **not a prevalence comparison**. It is a measurement/specificity result.

## Direct positive examples

### Asclepias exaltata

Prior or simultaneous self pollen strongly reduces subsequent outcross fruit and seed production.

This directly identifies a male-function product interfering with female outcross performance.

### Mimulus aurantiacus

Stigma closure moves the female organ out of the way of anthers.

Experimental prevention of closure reduces pollen export, while closure itself more than doubles export in the direct 2001 experiment.

This is a direct male-female interference and resolution experiment.

### Polemonium viscosum

Incompatible self pollen reduces compatible pollen germination and can reduce seed production.

The experiment provides direct evidence that temporal/spatial separation of pollen presentation from stigma receptivity can relieve interference.

### Eichhornia paniculata

Larger simultaneous floral displays increase geitonogamous selfing and reduce outcrossed siring success.

Experimental segregation of male and female function among flowers can reduce selfing and raise outcross siring success.

### Pontederia sagittata and Epilobium obcordatum

Pollen-chase experiments recover direct inhibitory effects of prior or simultaneous self pollen on later outcross seed set.

### Campsis radicans and Ipomopsis aggregata

The Barrett review places both among experimental systems showing reproductive costs of self pollen; they pass the first source-adjudicated conflict gate while remaining candidates for deeper primary-source extraction.

## Explicit specificity / null cases

### Pontederia cordata

Controlled tests found no significant effect of prior incompatible pollen on seed set and no evidence that stamens obstruct legitimate pollen receipt.

This is retained as a direct interference null rather than hidden.

### Turnera ulmifolia

The review similarly reports little influence of prior self pollination on seed set.

### Narcissus triandrus

Self-sterility and differential ovule development are biologically important but do not by themselves establish that one sexual function interferes with the other.

It therefore fails the strict interference gate under the current evidence contract.

## Unresolved architecture-heavy systems

Several systems have clear floral architecture but insufficient direct conflict evidence in the registered source:

- stigma-height dimorphism in *Narcissus assoanus* and *N. dubius*;
- flexistyly in *Alpinia kwangsiensis*;
- enantiostyly in four *Wachendorfia* species;
- historical heteranthery observations in *Solanum rostratum* and *Chamaecrista fasciculata*.

These systems are not discarded.

They are held for mechanistic/full-text adjudication because:

```text
architecture associated with outcrossing
!=
identified sexual interference
```

## Cross-universe implication

The current screening architecture now has two useful literature universes:

```text
U1 broad herbivore-pollinator interaction universe
   -> high specificity / mostly conflict-negative

U2 sexual-interference review universe
   -> enriched for direct conflict but still contains null and unresolved systems
```

This is exactly the structure needed for a defensible macro programme.

The analysis no longer begins with named positive BALANCE examples. It begins with externally defined literature universes and lets the conflict gate decide what survives.

## Claim ceiling

The U2 screen establishes source-adjudicated evidence for conflict presence/absence/uncertainty.

It does not yet establish:

- natural frequency of sexual interference;
- that a floral architecture evolved because of the measured conflict;
- theoretical BALANCE quantities such as `rho` or `d_B`;
- SLK accessibility or population-level evolutionary realization.

The next layer must separately code architecture and pre-existing module opportunity among conflict-positive groups.
