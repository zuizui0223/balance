**Date:** 2026-10-24  
**Status:** original-paper abstracts and one separately marked supplemental original publication; verified observations in a **prospective selection sample**, not an independently blinded A/B architecture code, a V4 outcome, population prevalence, or evidence that ecological conflict *caused a new structure to evolve*.

## What was actually executed

The first six taxa were selected **without inspecting floral morphology or architecture outcomes**: all three original U6 `SEQUENTIAL_WITHIN_UNIT` groups, then the lexicographically first three of the other eighteen `SIMULTANEOUS` U6 dependency groups.

The selection rule and original taxon universe are frozen in [the executable audit](../balance_domain/u6_original_source_ecology_pilot.py), which reads existing [21 groups](../data/BALANCE_PLANT_U6_PASS1_FREEZE_V1.json), [generic pre-outcome source packets](../data/BALANCE_PLANT_U6_PASS2_SOURCE_PACKET_V1.csv), and the frozen [63 predictor receipts](../data/BALANCE_PLANT_U6_CONFIRMATORY_PREDICTOR_RECEIPT_FRAME_V1.csv).

**Six original-source ecological observations are now recorded** in [a source-linked source-extractor-only table](../data/BALANCE_U6_ORIGINAL_SOURCE_ECOLOGY_PILOT_V1.csv). The other fifteen groups are explicitly `NOT_YET_EXTRACTED`. The **registered two independent coder rows per taxon (42 rows total)** are neither opened nor modified; those coders MUST use the original frozen generic source packet and remain blind to this post-selection investigator pilot until their own evaluations are locked. Nothing here can be counted as `CODER_A`, `CODER_B`, or independently adjudicated outcome.

## The real ecological observations

| Original source-selected group | Original source observation | Architectural/functional implication that does *not* overstate the evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Clusia arrudae* — sequential | [do Carmo et al. 2004](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1744-7429.2004.tb00329.x): honeybees remove **~99%** of pollen from male flowers; the legitimate resin-foraging bee *Eufriesea nigrohirta* exiting a honeybee-visited flower carries **less than 0.1%** of the pollen on bees exiting an unrobbed flower; female seed output is negatively associated with honeybee pollen-foraging pressure | **Direct source-backed ecological damage** of pollen theft on legitimate export. Independent [Carmo & Franceschinelli 2002](https://doi.org/10.1590/S0100-84042002000300011) reports the plants are dioecious; male flowers present **~66% of pollen on day one** of three. Species sex organization, pollen presentation schedule, and per-flower theft are different scales; no reconstruction of a new module or costed alternative |
| *Crescentia alata* — sequential | [Martínez del Río & Bullock 1990](https://doi.org/10.17129/botsci.1377): social bees collect pollen before flower-visiting bats; across **four observed trees**, earlier anther dehiscence was associated with more severe pollen theft | **Anther-exposure timing is ecologically relevant**, but these four-tree differences are observational, not a randomized gene/organ schedule intervention. Neither programmed independent whorl dosing nor novel structural modules are identified |
| *Tolmiea menziesii* — sequential | [Goldblatt et al. 2004](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00606-003-0067-1): flowers are **strongly protandrous**; *Gnoriste* fungus gnats are primary nectar-feeding pollinators with contact and transfer, whereas hoverflies and bumblebees typically rob pollen from **male-phase** flowers, after earlier legitimate visits | **Source-observed temporal male/female expression** is distinct from visitor chronology and does not measure cue-reversible phenotypic switching. Original source supports a timing/phase trait; whether this arose because of pollen theft is unknown |
| *Actinidia deliciosa* — simultaneous | [Kirk 1987](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2311.1987.tb00982.x): controlled thrips feeding shows roughly **0.2–0.7% of average floral pollen production consumed per thrips per day**, across source-study taxa | Direct per-thrips **pollen-loss assay**. Not an assay of alternative floral architecture, pollinator stigma contact, or plant fitness. The very **same article** also supplies U6's *Echium plantagineum* record, so these are **not two independent experiment programmes** |
| *Amomum subulatum* — simultaneous | [Sinu & Shivanna 2007](https://repository.ias.ac.in/49075/): a **narrow passage between anther–stigma column and labellum** plus a long nectar tube forces nectar-foraging *Bombus haemorrhoidalis* to contact pollen and stigma. Pollen-foraging *Apis cerana* removes pollen without touching the stigma | **Existing floral geometry filters visitor access and legitimate pollen deposition**. That is direct source-supported contact partition *without* proof that a new anther/gamete organ evolved from the conflict. Existing geometry and a new module are different claims |
| *Aphelandra golfodulcensis* — simultaneous | [McDade & Kinsman 1980](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1558-5646.1980.tb04033.x), original-study abstract reproduced by bibliographic index: large hermit hummingbirds provide pollination, but *Trigona* bees take pollen without pollinating; source reports heavy parasitism and style damage affecting roughly **40%** of flowers | **Severe antagonistic contact** but no source-confirmed architectural function partition from accessible article abstract; keep morphology and any adaptive origin `UNRESOLVED`. Its registered U6 generic recovery status remains `EVIDENCE_CEILING` |

The sources vary in identification strength. Source-publication abstracts permit **ecological observation extraction** but cannot substitute for the full methods/figures required by independent structural coders. For *Clusia*, the 2002 supplemental original study and the 2004 admissible pollen-theft source are explicitly **different articles**. *Crescentia* observations are indexed from the original journal record; no fabricated experimental intervention is assigned. This report does not claim to have read inaccessible copyrighted full articles or their raw tables.

## Biological knowledge gained while the independent structure labels are still absent

These six examples contain at least **three ecologically distinguishable mechanisms** that a single `pollen theft` or `flower morphological complexity` flag would collapse:

1. **Visitor-dependent contact/access filtering** — *Amomum* uses existing floral geometry to allow legitimate deposition for one visitor while permitting pollen consumption by another.
2. **Pollen-exposure timing and sex-phase organization** — *Crescentia* has anther dehiscence timing correlated with theft; *Tolmiea* has male/female floral phases; *Clusia* has multi-day pollen presentation **and** sexes separated among plants. These are different developmental/anatomical levels and are not automatically the cue-dependent reversible plastic policy of PAYOFF.
3. **Loss of male function through antagonistic pollen removal** — *Clusia* has a strong pollen-removal versus legitimate-transfer contrast; *Actinidia* assays insect consumption of pollen quantitatively. Neither thereby identifies separate anatomical reward versus pollen-transfer modules.

**Non-obvious source coding implication:** the U6 source-side predictor `module_substrate=SINGLE_OR_CONTINUOUS` is a description of the **pollen pool where the conflict begins**. It is **not** an observation that the entire species' reproductive organization is integrated. *Clusia* is actually **dioecious** yet retains a source-level pollen removal/transfer conflict at male flowers. A future empirical coefficient must distinguish these hierarchical levels rather than treat a shared pollen pool as an absence of structural division.

## Research units, untested fitness, and the next empirical gate

The original U6 universe still contains **21 biological taxon dependency groups**, but they are not 21 independent study programmes. Kirk (1987) supplies the shared admission reference `U6_REF_078` for both *Actinidia deliciosa* and *Echium plantagineum*, which this source audit now detects explicitly; source records for *Theobroma cacao* derive from two original references yet remain one taxonomic dependency group.

This **six-source pilot has no formal `SHARED`, `NONSTRUCTURAL_SEPARATION`, `STRUCTURAL_MODULE_DIVISION` or `MOSAIC` outcomes**. Its notes are observational ecology, deliberately blind-extractor-excluded from the official two-coder outcomes. In particular:
- The true amount of *same-coordinate simultaneous opposing functional optima* required for SCH `L` or PAYOFF `C` remains unmeasured.
- Matched alternative architecture `W_S^*, W_P^*, W_D^*`, independently measured module cost `K`, and the causal value margin `Phi` remain unmeasured.
- The developmental phenotype's within-genotype reversibility, heritability, genotype invasion, or independently dated ancestral origins remain unmeasured.

**Operational next action is more source observation extraction, not retrospective outcome imputation.** The independent A/B architecture reviews must proceed using only their frozen source packets, by reviewers distinct from the present source extractor. After locking them, the official V4 evidence gate must check outcome class common support, study/taxon independence and causal fitness comparability. Do not repair sparse source outcomes by importing known *Clarkia* or *Senna* division cases post hoc.
