# Pollen-stage denominator audit

Post-open follow-up to #284. Glechoma longituba (Zhang et al. 2007, DOI 10.1007/s11258-006-9244-y) explicitly distinguishes fraction pollinated, conditional stigma load and fruit production. The original article's full text (Methods and Results, author copy on Academia) reports stigma collection and separate tagged flowers followed to fruit. Its field results: 64.86% vs 83.33% pollination incidence, 15.96 vs 13.85 conditional pollen grains, and 58.89% vs 80.35% fruit formation (robbed/unrobbed). The source's reported sample sizes are not independent flowers or a common longitudinal cohort; do not derive exact binomial counts or causal mediation. Mean ratio of incidence and conditional load is not a stage-specific biological outcome. We require exact numerical correspondence to the four archived rows' relevant subset 384/385/387, report conditional-load-only, incidence-only, and exclusion sensitivities with all other rows unaltered. No claim of global metric harmonization.

For each original exact-context paper, compute min and max possible downstream minus upstream differences over all one-row selections, without guessing treatment identity; paper means of per-context extrema bound those selections. Sign invariance is a mathematical diagnostic, not verified matched treatments. Include all negative and positive cases.

Wu & Gao 2024, DOI 10.1093/jpe/rtad036, provide separate endpoint means/SEs from 43 removed-bract and 40 intact inflorescences. Reconstruct first-order log-ratio working variances from published SEs and Cauchy bounds on covariance. Report whether a negative contrast could be supported under any correlation scenario; unobserved covariance, plant clustering and treatment bundle limit inference.
