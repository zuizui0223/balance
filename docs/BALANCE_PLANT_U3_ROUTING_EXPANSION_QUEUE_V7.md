## Diascia terminal receipt

The fifth case, `Diascia anastrepta`, produced useful negative-control candidates but not a source-closed closest eligible control.

Close candidates were audited first:

- `D. megathura`: close to the case, but unlike fertile stamen cohorts -> heteranthery-absence FAIL.
- `D. purpurea`: described as closely related to the case, but reduced anticous vs fertile posticous stamens -> FAIL.

Three biologically eligible controls remain:

- `D. barberae`: four equal stamens; direct Rediviva oil-foraging/pollen-placement evidence.
- `D. cordata`: one undivided projecting fertile-stamen set; direct Rediviva pollination.
- `D. integerrima`: one undivided erect fertile-stamen set; direct Rediviva pollination.

However, the relative closest-eligible ranking among those three cannot be frozen from the public surfaces. NCBI contains no public nucleotide record for `D. anastrepta`; the source-published ITS topology is locatable but machine-readable figure retrieval is blocked; and the 1984 morphology group table alone does not order the eligible candidates relative to the case.

Therefore no convenient control is promoted.
