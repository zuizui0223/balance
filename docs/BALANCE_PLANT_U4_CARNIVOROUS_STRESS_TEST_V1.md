# BALANCE plant U4 carnivorous-plant pollinator-prey stress test v1

## Purpose

U4 adds a fourth biological literature family to the plant macro programme:

```text
pollination
versus
prey capture
```

The anchor is Jürgens et al. (2012), *Biological Reviews* 87:602–615, DOI 10.1111/j.1469-185X.2011.00213.x, together with high-information direct tests published before and after that review.

U4 is deliberately a **mechanism stress-test series**, not a prevalence denominator.

## Why this family is useful

Carnivorous plants make the competing functions unusually explicit:

- flowers use insects as pollen vectors;
- traps use insects as nutrients.

The same family contains:

- direct realized conflict;
- little/no realized overlap;
- spatial separation;
- chemical/signal separation;
- selfing-buffered pollinator capture.

It therefore tests whether the plant codebook can distinguish conflict from architecture rather than treating separation itself as proof of conflict.

## Registered species cases

U4 currently contains 10 species-level cases.

Direct positive conflict:

- *Pinguicula vallisneriifolia* — small pollinators are trapped and their loss contributes to pollen limitation; conflict varies with microenvironment.
- *Drosera hookeri* — pollinators constitute a large fraction of prey and flowers facilitate their capture; autonomous selfing buffers female-function cost.

No-demonstrated-conflict specificity cases include:

- *Drosera cistiflora*;
- *Drosera pauciflora*;
- *Drosera spatulata*;
- *Drosera arcturi*;
- *Dionaea muscipula*.

Unresolved ecological-overlap cases include:

- *Drosera auriculata*;
- *Drosera makinoi*;
- *Drosera toyoakensis*.

## Codebook repair — signal separation

U4 reveals a resolution mode that cannot be represented as purely temporal or spatial.

In *Drosera auriculata*, flowers can occur close to sticky traps, but flower and trap volatile blends differ. Floral odour attracts flower visitors while trap odour attracts prey.

The plant architecture codebook therefore adds:

```text
SIGNAL_SEPARATION
```

as a nonstructural architecture mode.

This category is useful beyond carnivorous plants because visual, olfactory or chemical channel separation can decouple ecological receivers without moving functions into separate organs or time periods.

## Selfing as a conflict buffer

*Drosera makinoi* and *D. toyoakensis* can capture major pollinator species while being strongly selfing.

U4 therefore distinguishes:

```text
pollinator-prey overlap
!=
demonstrated reproductive-fitness conflict
```

This is exactly analogous to the wider BALANCE principle that interaction or multifunctionality is not sufficient evidence of the target conflict estimand.

## Strong negative test

The Anderson (2010) comparison is especially useful.

*D. pauciflora* has a long scape and strong flower-trap separation, while *D. cistiflora* has a shorter floral display. Yet pollinators were not found trapped in either species, and flower height mainly improved pollinator visitation.

Thus:

```text
spatial separation
!=
proof that pollinator-prey conflict caused the separation
```

U4 retains the architecture state while refusing the causal promotion.

## Claim ceiling

U4 can show that the same function pair appears with different conflict states and different architecture modes.

It cannot estimate:

- natural prevalence of pollinator-prey conflict;
- a general causal effect of spatial/signal separation;
- theoretical R, K, Phi, rho, xi or d_B;
- SLK accessibility, invasion, fixation or occupancy.

All U4 rows remain SCREENED and primary-model ineligible.
