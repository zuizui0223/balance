# U6 pollen-theft universe: pre-outcome functional-opportunity and accepted-taxon audit

**Date:** 2026-10-21. **Status:** actually executed, pre-outcome source-only information; **NOT** new pollen-function observations, a fitted architecture response, an estimate of population prevalence or an adaptive/historical cause of organ evolution.

## Research question

To move beyond heteranthery-selected U3 positives and the nonexchangeable U2 review, the U6 [Hargreaves–Harder–Johnson (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-185X.2008.00074.x) pollen-theft literature universe was assembled **before inspecting visible floral architecture**. How much of a contemporary contrast of simultaneous pollen theft versus sequentially ordered exposure can this conflict-first sampling universe actually identify, while preserving taxonomic independence and original source uncertainty?

The answer is: **it provides an independent source-first comparison frame, but not yet a valid architecture-association coefficient**. The source code holds the predictors separate from the still-unstarted independent coding and does not read V4 confirmatory outcomes.

### The biological-source predictor opportunity is sharply asymmetric

| Source-side biological coordinate | Frozen source-screened U6 values | What this supports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `module_substrate` | `SINGLE_OR_CONTINUOUS` **21/21** | Pollen removal/retention conflicts use a common source pollen pool. **No predictor variation within U6**, not a claim that all flowers lack distinct organs |
| `conflict_spatial_geometry` | `SAME_UNIT` **21/21** | No source-side spatial predictor contrast within U6; destination flowers/pollinators are not source coordinates |
| `conflict_timing_geometry` | `SIMULTANEOUS` **18/21**, `SEQUENTIAL_WITHIN_UNIT` **3/21** | A potential future two-group contrast **only after** independent source adjudication and anatomy response coding |

The three **sequential consumer-exposure** sources are: `Clusia_arrudae` (pollen-removing honeybees precede effective native-bee visits), `Crescentia_alata` (social-bee theft precedes bat visits), and `Tolmiea_menziesii` (effective fungus-gnat visits precede pollen robbers). They are **ecological sequencing of visitors to a source pollen pool**, NOT demonstrated programmed delayed opening of specialized anthers of the kind studied in *Clarkia*.

**Post-open original-source chronology refinement:** a follow-on [replayable source ledger](BALANCE_U6_SEQUENTIAL_VISITOR_ORDER_V1.md) distinguishes the three original `SEQUENTIAL_WITHIN_UNIT` studies: **2** have pollen theft **before** effective pollen transfer (*Clusia arrudae*, *Crescentia alata*), while **1** reports effective fungus-gnat transfer **before** later pollen theft (*Tolmiea menziesii*). The distinction is original ecological visitor ordering, not a retrospectively added preregistered V4 predictor, anther developmental dosing state, trait architecture or evolution. *Clusia* supplies direct reported severe early pollen depletion and loss of subsequent native-bee body loads; the ~40% removal in *Crescentia* refers to **nectar**, not pollen. The late-theft study has no source-extracted comparable paternity effect. Neither order can be assigned an adaptive structural value without matched experimental fitness data.

**A necessary source-support condition is not a result.** The source-exposure indicator has two current categories, but its 18/3 support is highly uneven. There are only three potential sequential-side biological groups for any future timing comparison, even before taxonomy/phylogenetic dependence and independent coding. By contrast module substrate and source spatial geometry have one category each: no U6-only source-based predictor effect is identifiable from them **regardless of the eventual 21 architecture outcomes**. U6's source geometry also does not measure SCH opposed pure-function optima or PAYOFF concurrent-conflict floor `C`.

## Why this matters to the SCH–BALANCE–SLK/PAYOFF–BITA question

We now have **two complementary nonexchangeable selected source sets**:

- U3, sourced by visible heteranthery, documents genuine function-route diversity under morphologically similar and dissimilar anthers. *Clarkia* (Kay et al. 2020) adds source-supported **timed pollen dosing** as a rival to a different-function anatomical split.
- U6, sourced by directly recovered pollen theft **before opening floral architecture**, provides a more defensible source-defined background for looking at how an identical **pollen removal versus transfer** conflict can be spatially, temporally or structurally resolved. However, U6 has **no independently coded anatomy or phenotype outcomes yet**, and 21/21 source coordinates share one pollen pool.

This means none of these registered data can **yet** say whether a trade-off caused the **evolutionary origin of new floral architecture**. Even a future association between U6 pollen-theft exposure and anther architecture would require same-lineage fitness/cost comparisons and independently rooted nuclear/developmental history to establish causality.

### Real inferential blockers, in execution order

1. Retain and independently verify original Pass-2 coder A/B **architecture labels** for all 21 frozen U6 groups; **do not** substitute this audit's `SINGLE_OR_CONTINUOUS` source exposure tag for morphology or automatically merge Clarkia's source-selected positive cases.
2. Independently adjudicate 63 pre-outcome predictor receipts. The frozen 18/3 split is a *source-screen* signal, not final ecology.
3. Recompute source-block and accepted-taxon independence with the frozen taxon overlay before fitting any V4 outcome model; never count `Melastoma affine` and `M. malabathricum` as independent species-level transitions without explicit alternative taxonomic treatment.
4. Inspect four-class architecture support and overlap by time stratum **only after the independent coding is complete**, then declare whether a model is estimable, sparse or HOLD. No post-outcome replacement taxa.
5. Treat original `Clarkia` pollen dosing as developmental time-programming, not the same exposure coordinate as sequential pollinator arrival in U6, or a PAYOFF memory-driven reversible response.
