# Richman 2017: publication-point stage contrast (2026-11-24)

Original article DOI 10.1111/1365-2745.12657, 2013 whole-plant experiment. 40 plants assigned haphazardly per arm. C = no robbing, P = one primary robbery, PS = primary and repeated secondary (daily, mean 4 days). C/P have intact collars; PS has windowed collar. No secondary-only arm; repeated removal intensity and access route are bundled. No claim of factorial synergy.

The original text reports PS received 23% less pollen than P and 28% less than C, fruit success means 21.6% PS, 35% P, 53.5% control (the prose prints S, inconsistent with the only three registered treatments; provisionally C), seeds per mature fruit reductions 35% PS/P and 37% PS/C, total seeds 49% PS/P and 65% PS/C. From these rounded values, the fruit-minus-pollen lnRR difference is -0.22129 (PS/P), -0.57848 (PS/C), -0.35719 (P/C indirectly reconstructed). All depend on the same three group means. Not three independent effects; no matched uncertainty, raw data or causal transmission claimed. The PS/P total seed-minus-pollen difference is -0.41198.

Pollen: weekly destructive stigma sampling up to 3 per plant, six weeks, max 10 per plant; original model includes plant and week. Fruit endpoint is mature/(mature+aborted), not per original flower; seed endpoint undamaged seed/mature fruit. Visit observations used a separate set of 30 plants, and original visit treatment test p=.44. Do not treat these as observed individual path or claim equal visit rates.

For a source-verified reanalysis: preserve actual plant identities, treatment codes, units and missingness. Aggregate conspecific pollen within plant (also equal-week sensitivity) and merge only verified plant IDs to fruit/seed rows. Report all allocated plants, matched IDs and all losses by group. Joint within-arm plant-level resampling of the vector of outcomes estimates a sampling covariance and the contrast of two log response ratios. A plant-level comparison is not necessarily flower-level mediation. Report unadjusted descriptive contrasts plus any supported block sensitivity. Shared control covariance retained; don't pool three contrasts independently. No extrapolation to V4, structural architecture or male genetic paternity.
