# First six U6 pollen-theft plants: original-source clocks and floral mechanisms

**Date:** 2026-10-21. **Evidence state:** original-paper abstract/method observations across the **first six of 21 architecture-blind U6 taxa, in frozen order**. **Not** a completed CODER_A or CODER_B, no four-class `architecture_mode`, no source-independent adjudication, no full V4 data, and no macroevolutionary estimate.

## Why this matters

Our headline trade-off-to-new-architecture hypothesis has repeatedly risked conflating **different time coordinates**: simultaneity of competing pollen removal versus export at the source pollen pool; chronological arrival of different animal visitor guilds; successive florets opening; within-flower morphological male versus female phases; feedback that changes those phases' lengths; and pollen consumption by insects **measured only in the laboratory**.

The U6 conflict-first, outcome-blind review frame selected 21 plant groups before looking at organ morphology. This initial pass uses exactly **the first six frozen groups**, without replacement or outcome-filtered inclusion. It reads sources **for distinct ecological phenomena**, but deliberately **does not assign the original U6 independent architecture outcome categories or substitute new predictor decisions for the frozen 63 SCREENED receipts.**

## New positive biological interpretation: not all temporal responsiveness is reversible plasticity

The **strongest new within-source mechanistic receipt** is *Campanula americana*:

1. In the original greenhouse experiment, pollen removal changed the **male floral phase duration** and pollen deposition changed the **female floral phase duration**, while the same sexual function is expressed at different times.
2. The flower is already developmentally protandrous: anthers provide pollen to style hairs before/at anthesis, and stigmatic lobes open later. The response **shortens or extends a stage**; it does not demonstrate a mature female flower returning to a previous male phase.
3. The source reports that male/female **functional** pollen transfer/deposition saturates within hours of morphological phase onset, but **morphological** phase termination is delayed after this functional saturation.
4. A separate study shows the ecological siring cost of inefficient pollen visitors depends on the availability of effective pollinators, but these studies were not a crossed manipulated alternative architectural design.

This demonstrates a **real, within-genotype/within-flower developmental response class** for a candidate three-/four-strategy ecology: **cue-responsive but effectively irreversible ontogenetic phase progression**. It is neither preprogrammed and wholly insensitive to pollen cues, nor a Markov-like state that can switch back and forth without developmental cost. The empirical mechanism exists, while an evolutionary role in the *origin of the phase architecture* remains untested.

A similarly critical distinction holds for *Bromelia*: visitor arrival is ordered morning/afternoon but the flower's **structural function** may operate over a partly overlapping pollen/nectar exposure window. Opposing pollen fates at one source pool and non-overlapping **visitors** can both be recorded without any logical contradiction; they concern **different ecological clocks**.

## Precise next ecological test

For a single same-population lineage, independently record:
- pollen consumption/removal versus stigma deposition and *pollen siring* at the same visitor exposure window;
- **within-flower floral phase duration** and its response to experimentally removed/added pollen;
- timing of effective versus inefficient consumers, true environmental temporal correlation and pollinator contact;
- physically viable shared, cue-responsive developmental schedules, programmed pollen reservoirs and coexpressed functional-division alternatives;
- costs and combined male/female lifetime fitness on a common scale, and finally the independent historical developmental-genetic origin.

**Current causal ceiling:** real visitor-filtering and experimentally responsive pollen-presentation timing mechanisms exist and are different source-verified ecological options. There remains no evidence here that the pollen-theft trade-off historically **created a new heritable morphological module**.
