# BALANCE plant estimand-yield comparison v1

## Purpose

Compare what the first two outcome-blind plant literature universes contribute to the BALANCE estimand.

This is a comparison of **screening yield**, not biological prevalence.

## U1 — broad herbivory-pollination interaction universe

Current provisional first-20 source screen:

```text
20 source-ready records
13 excluded at S0:
   no single shared reproductive coordinate
7 retained through the shared-coordinate gate
0 promoted conflict-positive
1 aligned no-conflict
5 no-demonstrated-conflict
14 unresolved overall including S0 exclusions
```

The major result of U1 is estimand specificity:

```text
herbivory x pollination interaction
!=
shared-coordinate functional conflict
```

Many high-quality interaction experiments concern different organs, downstream effects or agent manipulations rather than one reproductive trait coordinate under opposing demands.

## U2 — targeted sexual-interference universe

The Barrett-2002 review universe is source-closed at 22 dependency groups.

Conservative provisional screen:

```text
7 conflict-positive
2 direct no-demonstrated-conflict/null cases
13 unresolved
```

The positive cases span multiple architecture modes, including:

```text
SHARED_INTEGRATED
TEMPORAL_SEPARATION
SPATIAL_SEPARATION
TEMPORAL_AND_SPATIAL_SEPARATION
```

This universe therefore supplies direct evidence that the comparative response should be architecture mode rather than structural split alone.

## What may be concluded

The current development data support:

> A mechanism-targeted review universe yields more directly identifiable shared-function conflict cases than a broad ecological-interaction review universe under the present BALANCE codebook.

This is useful for study design.

## What may not be concluded

Do **not** infer:

```text
sexual conflict is more common than pollinator-antagonist conflict
U2 taxa are more conflict-prone than U1 taxa
7/22 versus 0/20 estimates natural frequencies
```

The two reviews have different inclusion mechanisms and the U1 sample remains provisional pending full 47-taxon reconstruction.

## Design consequence

The confirmatory programme should combine literature universes only after:

1. source-defined membership is frozen;
2. biological dependency groups are deduplicated;
3. conflict is screened independently of architecture;
4. source universe/conflict family remain visible in models and sensitivity analyses.

The U1/U2 contrast is therefore a measurement lesson, not an ecological rate result.
