## The nontrivial ecological constraint

Pollen that is irreversibly stolen **before** an effective export opportunity is unavailable to the later visitor; pollen stolen **after** successful export cannot retroactively change the transfer that already occurred, although it may reduce further export during later visits. The expected reproductive consequence depends on baseline pollen reserve, export efficiency, flower age, visitor timing, pollen replenishment, pollen availability and subsequent visits.

Therefore:
- The original label `SEQUENTIAL_WITHIN_UNIT` does **not** determine the *direction or magnitude* of the impact on effective pollen transport.
- The observed *Clusia* pollen depletion and reduced bee pollen body load identify a biologically strong early-theft **ecological route**, not a genetic trait architecture response.
- *Crescentia*'s earlier-dehiscence/stronger-theft association makes developmental pollen exposure a candidate trait, but the four-tree comparison is an **association** and cannot establish that evolutionary selection moved anther timing.
- *Tolmiea*'s source establishes late robbers following effective gnats. No field outcome in this original source specifically demonstrates a zero fitness cost of late robbery.

An independent sequential-*source* distinction should therefore be `THEFT_BEFORE_EFFECTIVE_TRANSFER` versus `EFFECTIVE_TRANSFER_BEFORE_THEFT`, not a generic scalar "time separation reduces conflict."

## Relation to the Clarkia alternative and multi-repository theory

**Do not merge ecological visitor arrival order with floral programmed pollen dosing.** Original *Clarkia* Kay et al. (2020) report **two anther-whorl dehiscence schedules** as a possible pollen presentation strategy; these three U6 papers primarily describe *which external interacting agent comes first* at a pollen source. They use different biological clocks.

This distinction strengthens the existing SCH–BALANCE–SLK/PAYOFF–BITA programme:
- **SCH:** the observed visitor sequence is NOT two opposed pure-function `do(z)` fitness optima on a single measured structural coordinate. Functional conflict load L and concurrent-only floor C remain unidentified.
- **BALANCE:** the current ecological route does not compare viable shared, reversible, preprogrammed temporal and multi-axis different-function architecture fitness on the same pollen-siring/viable-seed horizon.
- **PAYOFF/SLK:** knowing visitation timing may alter payoffs on retained pollen and future transfer, but neither a Markov environmental flip rate `rho` nor heritable structure/memory switching cost `K` was measured. A module being intrinsically better is separate from being reached, invading or arising because of the conflict.
- **BITA:** external early/late consumer order can change accessible legitimate-versus-bypass reproductive routes. Clusia quantifies strong route damage to carrier pollen loads, but these effects are not the historical origination of new floral organs.

A future controlled comparison would randomize or match **legitimate-visitor versus pollen-consuming-visitor arrival order**, while holding flower age, pollen availability, number of visits, density and pollen production as constant as feasible, and measure viable siring rather than pollen-removal counts alone. Simultaneous manipulation of anther release would be a **different experiment**.

## Complementary source examples (not source-balanced controls)

In the same preselected U6 universe, published original studies of *Banksia menziesii* report staphylinid beetles remove substantial pollen yet deposit none onto stigmas (Ramsey 1988, [DOI 10.1007/BF00379609](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00379609)). Published *Campanula americana* experiments report low-efficiency halictid visits reduce siring when efficient *Bombus* visits are scarce, but not when high-efficiency visits are more abundant (Lau & Galloway 2004, [DOI 10.1007/s00442-004-1677-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00442-004-1677-1)). These **different ecological-effect measurements** suggest consumer effectiveness and pollinator community context as candidate modifiers. They were considered after reviewing examples and **cannot be counted as matched controls**, pooled fitness effects or independent module origins.
