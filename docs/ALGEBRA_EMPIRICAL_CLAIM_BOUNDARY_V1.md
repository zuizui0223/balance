# BALANCE algebra / empirical claim boundary v1

## Decision

BALANCE will not present identities obtained by solving the registered two-margin system as
independent biological results.

### Coordinate algebra — descriptive only

Under `R=sL`,

```text
L>0 and sL-K<0
<=> 0<L<K/s.
```

With

```text
rho=K-sL
xi=L/(L+rho)
d_B=min(L,rho),
```

the equal-margin point `L=rho=K/(1+s)`, `xi=1/2`, and the normalized width ratio
`1/s` are algebraic consequences of the registered coordinate system.

They remain useful for:

- reporting position and reserve on a common fitness scale;
- numerical regression tests;
- designing matched environmental sampling;
- comparing direct and decomposed representations.

They are not used as headline novelty claims.

## Empirical contribution

A BALANCE result must contain information not forced by the coordinate definitions.

Current admissible result classes are:

1. **direct worldline ordering** measured from matched `W_S*` and `W_D*`;
2. **bridge failure or concordance** when direct and decomposed routes are measured
   independently;
3. **cross-context rejection/support of registered shape restrictions** such as monotonicity,
   affine-envelope structure, or duration-dependent switching;
4. **comparative predictors of conflict routing/architecture** coded independently of the
   observed architecture outcome.

## Macro primary question

The preferred comparative question is:

> Given independently established functional conflict, what predicts whether the conflict
> remains in a shared architecture or is separated temporally, spatially, by signal channel,
> or structurally?

The plant-first confirmatory lane therefore prioritizes observable, pre-outcome variables:

```text
pre-existing module substrate
conflict timing geometry
conflict spatial geometry
independent developmental control when available
```

A predictor inferred from the focal architecture outcome is rejected as circular.

## Claim ceiling

Do not write:

- "we discovered the BALANCE interval `0<L<K/s`";
- "the deepest point theorem predicts `K/(1+s)`" as a biological discovery;
- "the `1/s` width ratio explains architecture evolution";
- "`xi` or `d_B` identifies historical transition probability".

Allowed wording is:

- "we use a two-margin coordinate in which...";
- "under the registered quadratic bridge, the coordinate implies...";
- "we then test the additional empirical restriction that...";
- "the comparative analysis asks which independently coded features predict architecture
  state among conflict-positive systems."

## Review defence

If challenged that the static geometry is obtained from two linear inequalities, the answer
is affirmative: that geometry is intentionally treated as bookkeeping. The scientific
burden is carried by direct measurement, independent bridge checks, prospective
cross-context restrictions, and the comparative macro analysis.
