# BALANCE theory -> reality-pattern recovery v1

## Primary sequence

BALANCE is ordered as:

```text
mathematical middle-world mechanism
-> observable signatures
-> systematic metadata / meta-analytic recovery
-> only then focal worldline experiment design
```

The empirical question is not initially whether one species directly identifies `Phi=sL-K`. The synthesis first asks whether systems with active conflict repeatedly occupy a persistent shared/integrated regime instead of immediately differentiating.

## Fixed theoretical object

BALANCE occupies the interior in which conflict exists but the shared worldline still wins:

```text
L > 0
Delta_W = W_D* - W_S* < 0
```

or under the registered decomposition:

```text
Phi = sL - K < 0.
```

Theory additionally predicts depth/reserve, environmental width/topology, critical boundaries, re-entry restrictions, and hysteresis when switching costs matter.

## Metadata-level observable signatures

For each independent biological cluster, code the strongest available evidence on:

```text
conflict_present
shared_or_integrated_architecture_present
alternative_or_differentiated_architecture_present
shared_state_persists_despite_conflict
relative_performance_or_fitness_when_available
environmental_or_geographic_context
boundary_or_transition_state
reentry_or_reversal
history_or_hysteresis_information
switching_or_reconfiguration_cost_proxy
study_design
source_verification_state
```

Primary pattern classes:

1. `CONFLICT_WITHOUT_SPLITTING` — opposing demands are present but one shared/integrated architecture persists.
2. `SANDWICHED_TRANSITION_MOSAIC` — shared, intermediate, and differentiated regimes occur along an environmental/geographic gradient.
3. `BOUNDARY_CROSSING` — evidence indicates a transition from shared to differentiated state as context changes.
4. `PERSISTENT_INTEGRATION_WITH_ALTERNATIVE_AVAILABLE` — an alternative architecture exists or is experimentally accessible but does not outperform the shared state in the focal context.
5. `HYSTERESIS_OR_PATH_DEPENDENCE` — forward/reverse transitions or historical dependence differ.
6. `DIRECT_DIFFERENTIATION` — conflict is followed by differentiation with no supported middle regime; useful boundary evidence.
7. `UNRESOLVED` — conflict or architecture state is known but relative worldline ordering is not.

## Synthesis targets

Recover:

```text
recurrence of conflict-without-splitting
frequency of transition mosaics among screened eligible systems
moderators associated with persistence versus splitting
width/duration/context of middle regimes where measurable
path dependence / hysteresis cases
negative cases where integration does not persist
```

Do not treat publication counts as natural prevalence. Quantitative pooling is restricted to compatible definitions and scales; otherwise use evidence mapping, ordered regimes, and cluster-level transition signatures.

## Claim ceiling

The synthesis can show that BALANCE-like persistence signatures recur and identify ecological moderators associated with the middle regime. It cannot estimate `s`, `K`, `Phi`, `xi`, `rho`, or `d_B` by combining unmatched studies, and cannot prove that persistence was generated by the registered BALANCE mechanism in every system.

## Final layer: focal empirical design

Only after the cross-system pattern map is mature should direct shared-vs-differentiated worldline experiments become the priority. Same-context common-fitness-scale measurements can then identify a bounded direct gap and test depth, boundaries, or hysteresis. Peucedanum/Pedicularis remain candidate validation systems, not prerequisites for the synthesis.
