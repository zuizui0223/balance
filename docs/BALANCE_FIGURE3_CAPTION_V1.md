# Figure 3 caption — source-adjudicated reality-pattern map

**Figure 3. Recurrence of BALANCE-compatible pattern classes across biological domains.**

Rows are source-adjudicated pattern classes and columns are biological domains. Circle size gives the number of independent biological clusters in each pattern-class × domain cell. The current ledger contains 17 independent clusters, of which nine are middle-regime signatures: seven conflict-without-splitting clusters, one persistent-integration-with-an-available-alternative cluster, and one sandwiched transition mosaic. Two additional clusters are classified as boundary crossings, one as direct differentiation, and five remain unresolved. Plant systems currently contribute most of the screened evidence, but middle-regime and architecture-boundary signatures also occur in protein, gene-regulatory, genome-architecture, and vertebrate-morphology domains.

The figure is a recurrence map within a screened, source-adjudicated evidence universe. Cell counts must not be interpreted as estimates of natural prevalence, relative biological frequency, or direct BALANCE occupancy. In particular, pattern-class recurrence does not identify optimized shared-versus-differentiated worldline fitness (`W_S*`, `W_D*`) or derived quantities such as `rho`, `Phi`, `xi`, or `d_B`.
