# BALANCE U3 conflict-conditioned routing readiness v2

## Why the next U3 question changes

Binary pollen-fate conflict presence does not deterministically distinguish heteranthery from nonheteranthery. All four cases are conflict-positive and three nonheterantherous controls are also conflict-positive. The unresolved Osbeckia state cannot make a finite matched binary conflict coefficient estimable.

The biologically useful target is therefore **routing architecture conditional on conflict**.

## Public-evidence ceiling is now frozen

Repeated retrieval is no longer treated as an open development gate.

The canonical ceiling ledger is:

```text
data/BALANCE_PLANT_U3_EVIDENCE_CEILING_V1.csv
```

Four unresolved targets are frozen at `PUBLIC_RETRIEVAL_CEILING_FROZEN_UNRESOLVED`:

1. species-level effective animal pollination for `Monochoria australasica`;
2. species-level effective animal pollination for `Monochoria cyanea`;
3. pollen-reward versus gamete-transfer conflict measurement for `Osbeckia chinensis`;
4. broader routing architecture for `Senna covesii`.

This status does **not** mean the biology is absent. It means the registered public-source audit did not recover evidence that satisfies the frozen gate.

For `M. australasica`, the best bee record is male territorial behaviour over water containing the plant, not a flower visit. For `O. chinensis`, bee handling and stigma contact are documented, but no quantitative pollen-removal/export or fitness contrast is recovered. For `S. covesii`, binary pollen-fate conflict is positive, but equal/weakly differentiated fertile-stamen morphology cannot be converted into `SHARED_INTEGRATED` because `S. spectabilis` already demonstrates that equal fertile-stamen morphology can coexist with positional within-flower routing.

## Consequence

The next evidence path is no longer:

```text
repeat the same literature search
```

It is:

```text
new direct primary source or new empirical measurement
OR
the frozen prospective routing-expansion queue
```

The prospective queue is now registered at `data/BALANCE_PLANT_U3_ROUTING_EXPANSION_QUEUE_V1.csv` and starts with `Amoreuxia wrightii` (Bixaceae). New controls must still be selected before conflict strength or routing architecture is extracted.

## Why no routing model is fit yet

Only two positive controls have resolved routing architecture, occupying two dependence blocks. No positive control is source-secure as `SHARED_INTEGRATED`.

No arbitrary minimum-n threshold is introduced after seeing these data. A prospective routing model and estimability contract must be frozen before any confirmatory routing fit.

## Claim ceiling

The ceiling audit records the limit of the registered public retrieval, not biological absence or exhaustive proof that no source exists.

The routing diagnostic identifies acquisition priorities; it does not estimate routing effects, population prevalence, conflict strength, or historical causation.
