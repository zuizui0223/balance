# Independent reproductive denominator and original-data expansion

Irwin 2009 (10.1093/aob/mcp056) reports a common garden in Ipomopsis aggregata, eight groups, low/high robbing. It defines fruit set as seed-bearing fruits divided by total flowers per plant. This is biologically different from Richman's pooled recorded fruit fate category. Original article reports 17% more pollen, 56% higher fruit set, 34% more seeds per fruit, 58% more total seeds under low robbery. High/low log ratio point differences based on these rounded values only; interval from printed rounding is not a sampling CI. The archived group rows are audited without assuming their order identifies a group. Same species/research context as Richman, not independent species validation. No raw plant data or matched covariance for Irwin 2009. See original https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2701760/.

Heiling et al. 2018 (10.1111/oik.05440) Corydalis caseana, different plant taxon, published tests of three commensalism hypotheses (non-discrimination, relative visitor frequency, pollen saturation); no claim of new discovery. Source DOI 10.5061/dryad.bh6hs70, workbook Heiling.et.al.Oikos.2018.data.xlsx, previously pinned Dryad MD5 31e7e67fa98c5ffb7b4b133686191b90. University of Arizona repository also identifies a Zenodo mirror. Recover only DOI/title/filename/checksum-matched public source; inspect sheet metadata and plant/raceme keys before fitting. No same-flower claim or new raw analysis until source admitted.

Independent additional candidate Digitalis purpurea, DOI dataset 10.5061/dryad.4j0zpc8c1, original report measures female seeds per fruit and male pollen export, not female pollen receipt. Do not use the male measure as an upstream female-fitness mediator. No V4 changes.
