# BALANCE macro predictor feasibility audit v1

## Purpose

Evaluate whether the current cross-domain pilot can support the registered comparative predictors without circularly inferring predictors from the architecture outcome.

This audit uses:

- `data/BALANCE_MACRO_PILOT_LEDGER_V1.csv`;
- `data/BALANCE_MACRO_PREDICTOR_RECEIPTS_PILOT_V1.csv`;
- the fail-closed receipt rules in `balance_domain/macro_predictors.py`.

No confirmatory model is fitted here.

## Pilot receipt result

The first receipt audit contains 15 source-specific predictor claims.

```text
ADJUDICATED independent receipts   8
SCREENED / unresolved receipts     2
REJECTED circular receipts         5
```

The explicitly rejected rows are scientifically important. They show where an apparently obvious predictor would have been derived from the response:

- current heteranthery cannot by itself prove that heteranthery was accessible before it evolved;
- current GAL1/GAL3 duplication cannot serve simultaneously as the outcome and as independent accessibility evidence;
- observed male-only flowers in `Pulsatilla` cannot by themselves establish prior module accessibility;
- absence of beak duplication in Darwin's finches cannot establish low historical accessibility;
- adult crusher/cutter mechanics cannot alone establish pre-split coupling.

## H2 is currently more observable than H1

Independent adjudicated functional-coupling receipts were recovered for:

- `Polemonium viscosum`: one corolla-shape system jointly affects pollinator attraction and ant-predation risk;
- Darwin's finches: direct force-velocity trade-off in one jaw apparatus;
- the HisA/TrpF system: direct mutational trade-off between two catalytic activities in one sequence;
- coGFP: green-versus-blue fluorescence trade-off in one sequence;
- `Pulsatilla alpina`: protogyny couples female-first function to delayed male function within bisexual flowers;
- the ancestral GAL system: reconstructed bifunctionality with an identified regulatory adaptive conflict.

Thus H2 has at least six pilot systems in which the same integrated architecture can be used to measure coupling before the resolution outcome is used as evidence.

This does not make the HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW scale measurement-invariant across domains.

## H1 remains difficult

Only two pilot systems currently have independently adjudicated accessibility receipts under the strict rule:

- `Homarus americanus`: paired claws begin equivalent and experimental rearing/exercise can direct crusher-versus-cutter differentiation;
- coGFP: one-copy and two-active-copy constructs were experimentally created before observing whether functional specialization occurred.

Several otherwise attractive H1 examples fail the independence test.

The broad cross-domain category `alternative_accessibility` therefore remains too judgment-heavy for a first confirmatory model.

## Consequence

Do not promote the original cross-domain model

```text
structural_differentiation
~ alternative_accessibility
+ functional_coupling
+ ...
```

to confirmatory status yet.

The current pilot supports three conclusions instead:

1. the response architecture can be coded across domains;
2. direct coupling can sometimes be measured independently of the response;
3. broad architectural accessibility is not presently a commensurate cross-domain variable.

## Measurement-invariance problem

The biological meaning of an 'accessible alternative' differs substantially among the three pilot domains:

```text
genes/proteins:
  duplication, expression control, mutational routes

paired/serial animal structures:
  homolog differentiation, developmental plasticity

plant reproductive structures:
  serial floral organs, repeated flowers, temporal/spatial sex-function separation
```

Treating those as one ordinal HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW scale would move biological judgement into the predictor and risk generating a result by coding convention.

## Programme decision suggested by the pilot

Retain the 55-system cross-domain set as:

```text
GENERALITY / MEASUREMENT-STRESS-TEST LAYER
```

rather than the first confirmatory denominator.

Develop the first confirmatory macro study within one biologically commensurate domain:

```text
FLOWERING-PLANT REPRODUCTIVE ARCHITECTURE
```

where function, module, environmental context and resolution mode can be coded on a common biological basis.

The cross-domain layer remains valuable for testing whether the resulting concepts travel beyond plants; it should not be used to manufacture a universal effect size.

## Why plants are currently the best confirmatory stratum

The plant literature already contains multiple, mechanistically distinct conflict-resolution families:

1. pollinator versus antagonist effects on one floral coordinate;
2. male versus female sexual interference within hermaphroditic flowers;
3. pollen-as-food versus pollen-as-gamete conflict and heteranthery;
4. pollinator versus prey capture in carnivorous plants;
5. aligned/no-conflict multifunctional floral controls.

These families jointly span:

```text
shared compromise
temporal separation
spatial separation
within-flower division of labour
among-flower modular divergence
context-dependent mosaic
```

while retaining a common reproductive-fitness and developmental context.

## Freeze implication

The cross-domain H1/H2 hypotheses are **not abandoned**.

They become generality hypotheses to revisit after a within-domain operationalization demonstrates that the predictor can be measured without reference to the response.

The plant confirmatory programme should prefer directly observable architectural opportunity variables over the generic accessibility score.
