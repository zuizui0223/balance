## Already emerging, scientifically meaningful distinctions

1. *Clusia arrudae* has exceptionally direct source evidence of **pollen depletion before legitimate transfer**, whereas its physiological reward to the pollinator is resin. Pollen reward to thieves and a different commodity to effective pollinators are **not interchangeable functional coordinates**.
2. *Crescentia alata* shows a source-supported, **observational** correlation between dehiscence timing and pollen theft in four trees. That is closer to testing **temporal mitigation of functional conflict** than merely detecting two anther morphs, but cannot establish selection on an evolved morphological timing programme.
3. *Grevillea barklyana* offers an original experimental finding where contemporary pollen removal imposes a cost by removing the *fallback* of delayed autonomous selfing under inadequate bird pollination. The authors' introduced-honeybee exposure timescale specifically argues **against** claiming a current honeybee-driven new floral organ origin.
4. The *Echium* and *Actinidia* lab thrips findings share **Kirk 1987** as an original source, so two biological taxon names must not create two independent evidence-study programmes.
5. *Hamelia* is a genuine unresolved-source ceiling, not a reason to replace this position in the frozen source universe.
