## Why U6 exists

The frozen outcome-blind U1/U2 surface currently contains no
`STRUCTURAL_MODULE_DIVISION` cases under the strict conflict gate. A bounded U1
structural-recovery queue was source-screened and yielded zero promotions.

The response categories will not be collapsed and U3 heteranthery-positive cases will not
be used as denominator repair.

U6 instead opens a new **conflict-first, architecture-blind** literature universe based on:

Hargreaves, Harder & Johnson (2009), *Consumptive emasculation: the ecological and
evolutionary consequences of pollen theft*, Biological Reviews 84:259-276,
DOI 10.1111/j.1469-185X.2008.00074.x.

The review is suitable because it is organized around pollen theft / consumptive
emasculation: pollen is removed as a reward or food but fails to make a commensurate
contribution to gamete transfer. Admission is therefore upstream of heteranthery or any
other architecture outcome.

## Scientific question

> Among plant systems in which pollen theft or strongly inefficient pollen collection is
> empirically demonstrated, which architectures are associated with retaining, temporally
> separating, spatially separating, or structurally routing pollen reward versus gamete
> transfer?

The review itself is not treated as evidence that any particular architecture is adaptive.

## Two-pass outcome-blind reconstruction

### Pass 1 — freeze the conflict-defined universe

Classify every reference in the anchor review without coding focal architecture.

Reference status:

```text
EMPIRICAL_POLLEN_THEFT_CANDIDATE
EMPIRICAL_BUT_NOT_POLLEN_THEFT
REVIEW_OR_THEORY
NONPLANT_OR_METHODS
UNRESOLVED
```

A reference becomes a biological U6 candidate only when the primary study identifies a
plant taxon and contains direct empirical evidence of at least one of:

- pollen removal by a visitor with no or inefficient stigma deposition;
- increased pollen removal coupled to reduced male or female reproductive success;
- visitor-specific pollen consumption/removal that reduces pollen available for transfer;
- an experimentally demonstrated reward-removal / gamete-transfer mismatch.

Do **not** inspect or record heteranthery, dichogamy, herkogamy, poricidal anthers, sexual
system, or other architecture variables during Pass 1.

### Pass 1 exclusions

Exclude references that provide only:

- general pollination theory;
- generic pollen limitation without a pollen-removal conflict;
- nectar theft/robbery only;
- floral morphology without a demonstrated pollen-theft or transfer-efficiency receipt;
- bee foraging behavior without a plant-level transfer or reproductive consequence;
- review membership alone.

A study can remain `UNRESOLVED`; difficult cases are not replaced.

### Taxon reconstruction

For each admitted primary study:

1. extract every plant taxon for which the qualifying pollen-theft receipt is explicitly
   supported;
2. merge duplicate reports of the same biological system into one dependency group;
3. retain population/context rows only when the pollen-theft state or architecture is
   genuinely context-specific;
4. preserve every source supporting the dependency group.

The deterministic record ordering is:

```text
normalized plant taxon
-> publication year
-> first-author surname
-> context identifier
```

### Freeze point

Pass 1 is closed only when every anchor-review reference has a classification and every
included empirical reference has a taxon/dependency-group mapping.

At that point:

- hash/freeze the included dependency groups;
- freeze inclusion/exclusion reasons;
- freeze the deterministic coding sample;
- **then** open Pass 2.

## Pass 2 — architecture and predictor coding

Only after Pass 1 freeze may coders assign:

```text
architecture_mode
module_substrate
conflict_timing_geometry
conflict_spatial_geometry
```

The same outcome-independent predictor receipt contract used by U1/U2 applies.

Architecture categories remain the frozen BALANCE plant categories; no U6-specific outcome
category is invented.

## Independence from U3

U3 is heteranthery-positive by construction.

U6 is admitted by pollen-theft evidence and therefore must contain whatever architecture
the conflict-defined source universe yields, including possibly no structural cases.

A U6 taxon that also occurs in U3 is one biological dependency group, not independent
replication. Cross-universe overlap is recorded before modelling.

## Role in the programme

U6 is **not** automatically appended to U1/U2 in one prevalence denominator.

Its first role is an estimability and generality test:

1. can a conflict-enriched but architecture-blind universe recover additional routing
   modes, including structural division;
2. do the same independently coded module/timing/spatial predictors remain meaningful;
3. does U6 overlap so heavily with U3 or U2 that it adds no independent information?

Only after reconstruction and dependence auditing can a pooled or hierarchical model be
specified.

## Falsifiers / stopping outcomes

U6 remains valuable if:

- it recovers no structural division, strengthening the conclusion that structural routing
  is rare under strict direct-conflict evidence;
- most review references fail the direct empirical pollen-theft gate, showing that the
  conceptual literature is much broader than the identifiable evidence surface;
- architecture/predictor coding is not reproducible;
- all structural candidates overlap U3 dependence blocks.

None of these outcomes licenses outcome-based replacement of references.

## Claim ceiling

Before Pass 1 freeze, U6 supports only a registered search/reconstruction protocol.

After Pass 1, it can describe the review-defined pollen-theft evidence universe.

Only after independent Pass-2 coding and dependence checks can it contribute comparative
architecture associations. It never estimates natural prevalence of pollen theft across
angiosperms.
