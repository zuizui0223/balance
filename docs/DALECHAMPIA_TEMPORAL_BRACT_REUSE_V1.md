# Dalechampia: an observed post-pollination benefit of retaining the shared bract

**Status: SOURCE-REPORTED QUANTITATIVE READOUT / POSITIVE SEED-MASS EFFECT / NOT STRUCTURAL BALANCE OCCUPANCY.**

This evidence moves the BALANCE programme beyond its dry structural-source eligibility inventory, but does **not** repair the missing matched S/D architecture worldline. In *Dalechampia scandens*, the same floral bract is deployed as a conspicuous pollinator advertisement during anthesis and becomes a green, photosynthetically active structure around the maturing seeds after pollination. An independently randomized post-pollination experiment supports a *benefit of retaining the organ* for early seed development. This is a **real measured outcome in the primary literature**, not a newly generated field dataset.

## Three distinct and non-exchangeable source lanes

| Source | Biological layer | Observed evidence | Strict limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Pérez-Barrales et al. 2013, Oikos](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-0706.2013.20780.x) | Conflict while attracting pollinators in 2006 wild Mexico | Larger bracts received more bee visits/pollen but also more seed-predator egg deposition; combined seed selection tended stabilizing | Observational selection; does not identify a same-fitness-scale SCH causal conflict budget L |
| [Pélabon et al. 2015, Functional Ecology](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.12427) | Post-anthesis carbon contribution and reproductive return in 2013 greenhouse | Removing or shading bracts after hand pollination reduced **four-week seed mass**, while bracts became more photosynthetically active on greening | Not full seed maturity, not male fitness, not an S/D architecture comparison; bract manipulation can alter microclimate/protection |
| [Pélabon et al. 2012, Annals of Botany](https://doi.org/10.1093/aob/mcs091) | Reward production in a different greenhouse cohort | Manipulated resin production did not detectably change seed count/mass; resin-gland/flower-removal contrast was small and imprecise | Nonsignificant maternal cost is not K=0, cost equivalence, or evidence about prior construction expenditure |

**Never concatenate the rows across these papers to estimate one plant's** `L`, `K`, `Phi`, `W_S*`, `W_D*`, or structural differentiation. They used different cohorts, environmental contexts, functions and endpoints. The established *D. scandens* conflict-without-splitting cluster is **not** counted anew for this staged study.

## Source-reported seed-mass effects (2015)

2015 Methods: 39 maternal plants, 33 with all four assigned treatments completed. Hand pollination preceded randomized bract shade/removal and cut-control treatment. Maternal plant was a random effect in the fitted analyses. Seeds were harvested **four weeks** after pollination, not at final maturity. The study reported no detectable treatment difference in seed *number* but detected differences in seed *mass*.

All signs below are **treatment minus unmanipulated control**, units mg **per seed**, as reported by the authors.

| Postpollination manipulation | Source estimate ± source SE (mg) | Approximate normal-reference 95% range (mg) | Percent of control mean (34.67 mg) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Bracts removed | **−1.34 ± 0.32** | [−1.9672, −0.7128] | −3.87% |
| Bracts shaded | **−2.03 ± 0.32** | [−2.6572, −1.4028] | −5.86% |
| Edge-cut control | +0.29 ± 0.32 | [−0.3372, +0.9172] | +0.84% |

The ranges are computed from rounded published estimate ± **1.96 × reported SE** and are **NOT** the exact original mixed-effects model confidence intervals. They ignore the unknown model degrees of freedom and correlation between treatment contrasts, so do not infer a precise shade-versus-removal difference or treatment equivalence from them. Their role is to make the reported precision transparent, not to manufacture new inferential significance.

The directly measured gross *area-based* photosynthetic mean of green bracts was 1.47 ± 0.23 μmol m⁻² s⁻¹ versus white bracts 0.42 ± 0.24; their ratio of reported means is **3.50**. This ratio is only descriptive: with no reported cross-stage covariance, it has **no valid reconstructed uncertainty interval**. Leaves averaged 5.80 ± 0.25 on the same area-based scale. The original source's carbon-budget model estimated that green-bract photosynthesis could meet the blossoming structure's respiratory needs under an assumed 12-h moderate-light day, but it did not directly trace individual labelled carbon atoms into seeds.

2015 source: [full text, Results and Methods](https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/1365-2435.12427).

## Separate resin cost evidence (2012)

2012 Table 3 compared removal of the staminate inflorescence **plus gland** (arm 1) against removal of staminate flowers **while leaving the gland** (arm 2). This comparison was intended as a blossom-level estimate of resin-gland investment effects. The reported seed-mass difference was **−0.66 ± 0.54 mg**, n=51. Its approximate normal-reference range is **[−1.7184, +0.3984] mg**.

The data do **not** establish that reward production was costless, not least because prior developmental expenditure on the gland could not be removed after the organ had formed. The 2012 hand-pollinated seed-size test also excludes full male fitness and pollinator service, and it is not the same experiment as 2015. Neither study recovers `K` on a matched net architecture scale. The 2012 authors also reported a positive **across-individual** association between relative resin secretion and seed mass (r=0.40, 95% CI [0.16,0.60], n=56), consistent with a possible condition dependence, not a causal proof of absent within-individual resource costs.

2012 source: [Pélabon et al. 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3359932/).

## Positive biological conclusion, with its proper ceiling

**Supported:** an intact floral organ can deliver a later reproductive benefit after its conspicuous pollination-stage function has finished. The 2015 bract treatments reduced early seed mass and gas-exchange assays independently support a greening-associated carbon-contribution pathway. The organ is not simply a developmentally redundant floral advertisement after pollination.

**Adjacent, not combined:** the 2013 observational fitness map established a distinct early-stage bract-size conflict between mutualists and antagonists in a wild population.

**Not identified:** whether green bract carbon fixes exactly the difference in seed mass (no labelled-carbon transfer), whether an integrated bract architecture is better than an independently differentiated structural alternative, whether retaining it yields net lifetime male+female fitness, or whether this temporal reuse explains persistence under direct L>0 and Delta_architecture<0. This is a **staged-function-retention signature**, *not* a confirmed structural BALANCE domain or new independent recurrence.

The staged-functional benefit may raise the costs of losing existing floral organs, but must **not** be inserted numerically as `K` or `rho` in the SLK/BALANCE architecture-value equation.

## A falsifiable follow-up ecological hypothesis

**H_temporal_subsidy:** where an intact, display-bearing bract survives pollination, a later photosynthetic subsidy can increase seed developmental performance enough to contribute to the maintenance of that organ **even when its early pollinator-attracting size also increases exposure to seed predators**.

This is a *prospective explanation for persistence*, distinct from the tautological definition of the BALANCE region.

A rigorous first experiment would manipulate post-anthesis **photosynthetic availability**, with transparent/light-excluding sham covers matched for handling and microclimate, while leaving the bract's physical protection and early display intact. Randomize at the independent plant/compartment level and account for correlated blossoms. Cross this with independently validated predator exposure and, if feasible, prospective variation in early bract advertisement. Collect:

- pre/post light, gas exchange, surface temperature, water status, and bract closing integrity;
- pollen receipt/visitation and independently measured predator egg/attack counts;
- labelled `13C` assimilation and subsequent seed enrichment **if carbon transfer itself is to be claimed**;
- mature viable seeds, germination and seed mass, with plant IDs and losses retained.

A convincing carbon-pathway test predicts **lower seed performance under photosynthesis suppression with unchanged physical protection and environmental handling**, plus isotope transfer into seeds. If seed effects disappear after thermal/protection controls, the photosynthesis-specific explanation is falsified or remains unresolved; if a direct carbon transfer is not shown, retain a broader bract-retention benefit rather than labelling its pathway.

A strict **structural** BALANCE experiment is still a separate step: identify viable architecture S and genuinely different architecture D, common net fitness (including siring and female reproduction or a justified scalarization) and joint uncertainty in the **same population/season/ecological context**. Do not call the 2015 shade/removed bract states `S` and `D`: they are manipulation states of an already existing architecture.
