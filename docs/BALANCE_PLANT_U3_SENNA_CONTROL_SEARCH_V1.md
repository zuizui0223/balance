# BALANCE plant U3 Senna replacement-control search v1

## Purpose

Make the failed Senna negative-control search explicit rather than replacing the rejected control with a convenient distant species.

Both registered Senna cases lost their original proposed control because Senna surattensis has heteromorphic stamens and therefore does not satisfy the negative-architecture gate.

The replacement search asks two separate questions:

1. are the closest published relatives nonheterantherous?
2. if not, which source-secure homantherous Senna species could be considered after phylogenetic-distance and animal-pollination gates are closed?

## Close-relative result

### Senna alata

Recent plastome evidence recovers a close relationship between S. alata and S. siamea.

However, the comparative stamen study of Luo et al. documents strong stamen differentiation in S. siamea.

Therefore S. siamea is excluded despite strong phylogenetic proximity.

### Senna bicapsularis

The strongest recent phylogenetic result recovers S. corymbosa as sister to S. bicapsularis.

But S. corymbosa has direct experimental and field evidence for nutritional versus reproductive stamen roles and buzz pollination.

Likewise, close relatives S. occidentalis and S. siamea are heterantherous.

Thus the nearest well-supported relatives currently fail the architecture-negative gate.

## Open homantherous candidates

The broader Senna literature confirms that homomorphic or nearly homomorphic fertile-stamen architectures exist.

Three source-secure candidates are retained without promotion:

- Senna racemosa — seven fertile stamens described as homomorphic except slight abaxial diminution;
- Senna pumilio — fertile stamens described as homomorphic except slight distal accrescence;
- Senna villosa — clade VIIb, for which fertile stamens are approximately the same length and shape.

These rows remain CANDIDATE_OPEN.

Why they are not selected:

- the nearest-eligible phylogenetic ranking relative to each case is not yet frozen;
- effective animal pollination is not source-secure for the exact candidate species under the current evidence contract;
- pollen-resource visitation alone is weaker than confirmed effective pollination.

## Scientific implication

The difficulty finding a close negative comparator is biologically informative.

In both Senna case neighborhoods, strong androecial differentiation appears common among the nearest documented relatives.

This means a naive matched analysis could manufacture a contrast by jumping to a distant homantherous congener.

The protocol therefore keeps the Senna pairs failed/open rather than trading phylogenetic comparability for the desired architecture contrast.

## Next gate

For each open homantherous candidate:

1. place it on the same modern Senna phylogeny as the focal case;
2. rank phylogenetic distance among all source-secure nonheterantherous candidates;
3. recover direct effective-pollinator evidence;
4. apply the frozen source-quality and phylogenetic-distance tie-break;
5. only then register a replacement primary control.

## Claim ceiling

This search ledger documents candidate elimination and remaining options.

It does not establish that heteranthery is phylogenetically conserved around the focal cases, and it does not select a replacement control.
