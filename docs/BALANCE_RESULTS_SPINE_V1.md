# BALANCE Results spine v1

## R1. Conflict-compatible shared-coordinate patterns recur, but are not inevitable

The strict Q1B layer contains three independent effect-size-ready positive biological clusters (Fragaria vesca, Impatiens capensis, Gymnadenia conopsea 2015). The first allowed componentwise random-effects pool preserves the registered four-context contrast vector. Point estimates are directionally compatible with pollinator-favouring versus antagonist-opposing selection, but all modified Knapp-Hartung 95% intervals include zero at k=3. Two components show moderate heterogeneity (~63-66% I2). The inference is replicated directionally compatible conflict with context dependence, not a universal non-zero mean effect.

Design-matched negative controls (Trifolium repens, Lythrum salicaria) show that comparable factorial designs do not inevitably produce same-trait opposition. These controls constrain the domain of the mechanism rather than counting as failed positives.

## R2. Related conflict structures occur outside the strict Q1B estimand

Ipomopsis is retained as a sequential-filter boundary: pollination-stage selection can be strong while seed-predation selection on the same trait is not detected. Primula farinosa is retained as a discrete-morph boundary: pollinator and grazer selection oppose each other on a genetically based morph contrast, but that estimand is not a continuous standardized beta. Gymnadenia 2019 is an attribution boundary: statistical opposition exists, but the residual nonpollinator component is not identified as an antagonist.

Together these cases show why recurrence of numerical opposition alone is insufficient. Estimand compatibility and agent attribution are part of the empirical definition.

## R3. The broader literature contains recurrent middle-regime signatures

The source-adjudicated BALANCE pattern ledger currently contains 17 independent clusters, including 9 middle-regime signature clusters. These include conflict-without-splitting, persistence with an available alternative, transition mosaics, and boundary-compatible cases. This layer supports recurrence of persistence geometry across systems, not natural prevalence and not direct optimized shared-versus-differentiated fitness differences.

## R4. The direct BALANCE worldline remains unidentified

No current literature receipt directly compares an optimized shared architecture and a registered differentiated alternative on the same fitness scale and context with joint uncertainty. Therefore W_S*, W_D*, rho, Phi, xi, and d_B remain outside the empirical claim set. The literature synthesis narrows the final empirical target rather than replacing it.

## Results logic

The empirical synthesis therefore has four nested levels:

1. strict quantitative conflict recurrence (Q1B positives);
2. specificity controls and estimand/attribution boundaries;
3. broader persistence-geometry recurrence in the pattern ledger;
4. a still-empty direct worldline reserve.

This ordering mirrors the theory: conflict is a prerequisite, persistence geometry is the target phenomenon, and direct BALANCE occupancy requires matched worldline evidence that the literature synthesis does not yet provide.
