# BALANCE Discussion spine v1

## D1. The empirical signal is recurrence of conflict, not a universal average effect

The first quantitative Q1B pool should not be read as a search for one universal antagonistic-selection coefficient. Three independent positive systems reproduce the same qualitative conflict architecture, while the pooled confidence intervals remain wide and two components show substantial heterogeneity. At the same time, two design-matched negative controls fail to show the strict same-coordinate pattern. The strongest empirical conclusion is therefore that conflict-compatible selection recurs but is conditional, not inevitable.

This distinction matters for BALANCE because the theory is explicitly contextual. The sandwiched regime is defined by relative worldline advantage under a given combination of functional demands, not by a fixed global selection coefficient. Variation among systems is therefore partly signal: changes in antagonist identity, focal trait class, reproductive fitness component, pollination manipulation, and evidence source can change the effective weighting of competing functions. The moderator set is frozen before further cluster expansion so that this context dependence can be examined without post hoc explanation of the present heterogeneity.

## D2. Conflict is a prerequisite; persistence is the BALANCE phenomenon

A central conceptual risk is to equate multifunctional conflict with BALANCE itself. The Q1B layer only establishes the prerequisite: opposing agent-mediated selection can act on the same biological coordinate. BALANCE makes a stronger claim. It concerns systems in which conflict remains active while a shared or integrated architecture persists instead of immediately yielding to a differentiated alternative.

The broader pattern ledger addresses this second layer. Across 17 independent clusters, nine express middle-regime signatures, including conflict without splitting, persistent integration despite an available alternative, and a sandwiched transition mosaic. These observations do not measure `rho` directly, but they show that the qualitative geometry required by BALANCE is not confined to one focal taxon or one type of floral interaction. The empirical object is recurrence of persistence geometry, not the frequency of any one named mechanism in nature.

## D3. Negative controls and boundary cases define the domain of the mechanism

The synthesis gains information from systems that do not enter the positive numerator. Trifolium and Lythrum show that comparable factorial designs need not yield same-coordinate opposition. Ipomopsis shows that pollination and antagonism can act sequentially without producing the strict simultaneous Q1B estimand. Primula farinosa shows strong opposing selection on a discrete genetic morph, demonstrating that biologically relevant conflict can fall outside a continuous-gradient formulation. Gymnadenia 2019 shows that numerical opposition does not identify an antagonist when the second biological agent is unresolved.

These cases are not failed positives. Together they define the empirical domain of the theory. They separate absence of the focal mechanism from changes in estimand, temporal ordering, or causal attribution. Keeping these layers explicit prevents apparent replication from being manufactured by pooling biologically different objects.

## D4. BALANCE occupies the middle between conflict theory and differentiation theory

The results place BALANCE naturally between theories of conflict and theories of functional differentiation. Shared-coordinate conflict establishes a loss or tension that differentiation could in principle release. BALANCE asks why that release is not always realised: a shared architecture can remain favoured when the recoverable benefit of differentiation is insufficient to offset the costs, constraints, or lost coordination associated with separating functions. A transition occurs only when the relative worldline advantage changes sign.

This middle-regime framing changes the empirical question. The relevant contrast is not simply whether antagonistic selection exists, nor whether differentiated structures can be found. It is whether systems can remain in an integrated state despite active conflict, and how that persistence changes across environmental or functional contexts. The pattern ledger supplies recurrent examples of that middle regime, while the boundary cases show that neighbouring biological systems can occupy different estimand or transition structures.

## D5. The current literature identifies the pattern but not the direct worldline advantage

The main unresolved quantity is now sharply defined. None of the current literature receipts jointly compares an optimised shared architecture and a registered accessible differentiated alternative on the same fitness scale, in the same context, with joint uncertainty. Thus the synthesis does not identify `W_S*`, `W_D*`, `rho = W_S* - W_D*`, `Phi`, `xi`, or `d_B`. The literature establishes recurrence of the prerequisite and of the qualitative persistence geometry, but not strict BALANCE occupancy for a focal natural system.

This is an identification gap, not a reason to move the empirical experiment to the front of the paper. The literature synthesis should first establish what natural patterns recur, which estimands are compatible, and which comparisons existing studies systematically omit. Only then does a focal experiment have a well-defined role: to measure the matched shared-versus-differentiated worldlines that the literature cannot recover.

## D6. Implications for the SCH–BALANCE–BITA sequence

The three-theory sequence can therefore be kept conceptually clean. SCH asks when one coordinate is pulled by incompatible functional demands and thereby establishes the conflict geometry. BALANCE asks when that conflict persists without immediate splitting, defining the middle or sandwiched regime. BITA asks when differentiation or dimensional release becomes favourable and how functional routes reorganise after the boundary is crossed.

The empirical strategy can mirror the same order. First recover recurring conflict signatures; second recover persistence, negative controls, and transition boundaries; third recover differentiation-compatible route patterns. Direct focal experiments are reserved for the remaining identification gaps within each theory rather than being used as the initial empirical justification for the theory itself.

## Discussion claim ceiling

The Discussion may claim:

- recurring same-coordinate conflict under compatible quantitative designs;
- design-matched negative cases showing that conflict is not inevitable;
- recurrent middle-regime / persistence signatures in a source-adjudicated literature ledger;
- context dependence as a biologically meaningful feature to be tested with preregistered moderators after sufficient independent replication;
- a specific literature-wide identification gap in matched shared-versus-differentiated worldline fitness.

It must not claim:

- natural prevalence from the screened literature counts;
- a universal non-zero Q1B mean effect at `k = 3`;
- direct empirical identification of `rho`, `Phi`, `xi`, `d_B`, `W_S*`, or `W_D*`;
- that every boundary or sequential-filter case belongs to the strict Q1B estimand;
- that literature recurrence alone proves a focal natural system occupies BALANCE.
