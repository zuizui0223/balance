## Exposure-geometry clarification

Timing and spatial geometry are defined from the state in which conflict is **exposed**,
not from the focal resolution architecture.

Allowed:

- an integrated baseline;
- an experimental self/cross pollen challenge;
- a manipulated "resolution disabled" state;
- direct localization of interacting pollen, organs, flowers, or populations.

Not allowed:

```text
protandry observed -> SEQUENTIAL predictor
herkogamy observed -> spatial predictor
flexistyly observed -> timing predictor
sexual segregation observed -> between-module predictor
```

unless an independent baseline/challenge establishes the geometry.

### Timing geometry

Outcome-independent SCREENED timing geometry is currently recoverable for all 8 / 8.

- *Campsis radicans*: `MIXED` — self pollen was applied together with or before cross
  pollen.
- *Asclepias exaltata*: `MIXED` — self-before-cross, simultaneous self+cross, and
  cross-before-self treatments were all included.
- *Mimulus aurantiacus*: `SIMULTANEOUS` — the experimentally exposed female-organ
  interference with pollen removal/export occurs during the pollinator visit when the
  stigma is prevented from closing.
- *Polemonium viscosum*: `SEQUENTIAL_WITHIN_UNIT` — incompatible self pollen precedes
  compatible pollen at registered delays.
- *Eichhornia paniculata*: `SIMULTANEOUS` — the integrated baseline uses adichogamous,
  simultaneously hermaphroditic flowers.
- *Pontederia sagittata*: `SEQUENTIAL_WITHIN_UNIT` — self pollen precedes compatible
  pollen by 2, 4, or 6 hours.
- *Epilobium obcordatum*: `MIXED` — pollen-chase tests include prior and simultaneous
  self-pollen exposure.
- *Ipomopsis aggregata*: `SIMULTANEOUS` — the registered conflict exposure uses mixed
  self/outcross pollination.

These values describe the interference challenge, not observed dichogamy/herkogamy.

### Spatial geometry

Outcome-independent SCREENED spatial geometry is currently recoverable for all 8 / 8.

Seven systems expose the conflict through the same flower/pollen-pistil unit and are
screened as `SAME_UNIT`.

*Eichhornia paniculata* is different: its geitonogamous conflict is generated by movement
among repeated flowers within one inflorescence and is screened as `BETWEEN_MODULES`.

### Module substrate

The current first-pass source packet licenses only one module-substrate value without a
new dedicated morphology/substrate extraction:

```text
Eichhornia paniculata -> REPEATED_FLOWERS
```

The 2000 experiment explicitly starts from inflorescences made of repeated simultaneously
hermaphroditic flowers and then experimentally redistributes sex function among those
flowers. Thus the repeated-flower substrate exists in the integrated baseline and is not
defined by the imposed alternative.

For the remaining seven conflict-positive systems, module substrate stays `UNRESOLVED`
at this stage. Basic floral anatomy is available, but the existing categories
(`SINGLE_OR_CONTINUOUS`, `SERIAL_WITHIN_FLOWER`, `MULTILEVEL`, etc.) cannot yet be
assigned reproducibly without a dedicated substrate codebook audit.

## Design consequence

The current bottleneck is not timing or spatial conflict geometry. It is the operational
definition of **module substrate**.

The next methodological task is therefore not to gather more architecture outcomes. It is
to make module substrate objectively codeable from outcome-blinded morphology/development
evidence.

A successful repair should satisfy all three conditions:

1. two independent coders can reproduce the category;
2. the category can be assigned without knowing `architecture_mode`;
3. the category describes an opportunity/substrate that exists before crediting the focal
   resolution.

Until that repair is demonstrated, broad claims that "pre-existing modules predict
structural division" remain preregistered hypotheses rather than estimable confirmatory
results.
