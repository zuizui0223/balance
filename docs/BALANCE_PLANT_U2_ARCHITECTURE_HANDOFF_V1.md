# BALANCE plant U2 architecture handoff v1

## Purpose

Separate three claims that are easy to collapse:

~~~text
1. functional conflict exists
2. an architecture is observed
3. the architecture causally relieves the measured conflict
~~~

The U2 strict screen identifies eight direct conflict-positive dependency groups.

This handoff asks how much architecture evidence actually accompanies those eight cases.

## Result

Among the eight positive-conflict groups:

~~~text
direct causal/natural resolution experiment     1
direct experimental alternative architecture   1
natural architecture concordant but not causal 2
architecture unresolved                         4
~~~

Therefore:

~~~text
direct conflict evidence
!=
direct resolution evidence
~~~

even inside a literature universe explicitly devoted to sexual interference.

## Mimulus aurantiacus

Natural architecture:

~~~text
SPATIAL_SEPARATION
~~~

The bilobed stigma closes after pollinator contact and moves away from the anthers.

Experimentally preventing closure reduces pollen export; natural closure more than doubles export relative to the prevented-closure treatment.

This row is therefore:

~~~text
DIRECT_CAUSAL_RESOLUTION
~~~

It is currently the cleanest U2 example linking measured male-female interference to a specific architecture and relief of the measured conflict.

## Eichhornia paniculata

Natural experimental baseline:

~~~text
SHARED_INTEGRATED / adichogamous multi-flower display
~~~

The natural shared display exhibits geitonogamous pollen discounting.

Harder, Barrett & Cole experimentally rearranged sex function among flowers.

A male-below/female-above versus female-below/male-above arrangement changed selfing and outcross siring, showing that sexual segregation can relieve the mating cost under appropriate pollinator behaviour.

Because the separated architecture was experimentally constructed rather than simply read from the natural state, it is coded separately as:

~~~text
natural_architecture_mode       = SHARED_INTEGRATED
experimental_alternative_mode   = AMONG_FLOWER_MODULE_DIVISION
resolution_causal_status        = DIRECT_EXPERIMENTAL_ALTERNATIVE
~~~

This prevents an experimental counterfactual from being misreported as an evolved natural architecture.

## Polemonium viscosum

Natural flowers segregate pollen presentation and stigma receptivity in both space and time.

The self-pollen experiment shows direct interference, and the natural separation is directionally consistent with relief of that conflict.

However the architecture itself was not directly manipulated in the registered source.

It is therefore:

~~~text
TEMPORAL_AND_SPATIAL_SEPARATION
CONCORDANT_NOT_CAUSAL
~~~

rather than direct resolution evidence.

## Pontederia sagittata

Natural tristyly supplies spatial segregation among sex-organ positions.

Prior self pollen reduces later outcross seed set most strongly in long-styled flowers.

This makes the architecture and conflict biologically concordant, but the polymorphism was not directly manipulated as the conflict-relief intervention.

It is also:

~~~text
CONCORDANT_NOT_CAUSAL
~~~

## Four direct-conflict systems remain architecture-unresolved

- Campsis radicans
- Asclepias exaltata
- Epilobium obcordatum
- Ipomopsis aggregata

Their experiments identify reproductive interference, but this handoff does not infer a resolution architecture from generic knowledge of floral biology.

That fail-closed choice is deliberate.

## Macro consequence

The plant macro programme now has an explicit empirical ladder:

~~~text
broad interaction universe
-> strict conflict-positive systems
-> observed architecture
-> causal resolution experiment
~~~

U1 mostly fails before the conflict step.

U2 enriches direct conflict but still loses most cases before causal resolution.

This tells us what the macro database must actually collect: not merely examples of floral specialization, but systems where conflict and architecture are independently observable.

## Next evidence target

To populate the architecture side without selecting famous success stories, use a review-defined U3 architecture universe and retain both differentiated architectures and shared/undifferentiated comparator states.

For heteranthery, the appropriate unit should be a taxon-level comparative/phylogenetic frame rather than a list of hand-picked heterantherous species.

## Claim ceiling

This handoff supports a descriptive hierarchy of evidence.

It does not establish historical origin causation for architecture, natural prevalence, theoretical BALANCE depth, or SLK evolutionary realization.
