# BALANCE plant U3 targeted measurement contract v1

## Purpose

The remaining U3 evidence ceilings are now prospective measurement problems rather than open-ended literature searches.

This document freezes **what would count as sufficient evidence before any new result is seen**.

Canonical machine-readable contract:

```text
data/BALANCE_PLANT_U3_TARGETED_MEASUREMENT_SPEC_V1.json
```

## Monochoria controls

Both `M. australasica` and `M. cyanea` currently fail only the species-level effective animal-pollination eligibility gate.

Two evidence routes are admissible.

### Route A — single-visit transfer

Required:

1. exact focal plant identity;
2. animal flower visit;
3. reproductive-organ contact or pollen manipulation;
4. conspecific pollen deposition on the stigma attributable to that visit.

### Route B — visitor-access reproductive effect

Required:

1. exact focal plant identity;
2. animal-access versus exclusion, or a controlled single-visit treatment;
3. fruit or seed outcome linked to animal access/visitation.

The following remain insufficient:

- Amegilla territorial behaviour over plants;
- general Monochoria/Pontederiaceae buzz-pollination statements;
- morphology without a visitor;
- a visitor record without pollen transfer or reproductive effect.

If both candidates pass, the already frozen 68-CDS proximity result selects `M. australasica` before `M. cyanea`.

## Osbeckia chinensis

Two evidence routes are admissible.

### Route A — same-visit pollen-fate partition

Measure:

1. pollen removal from anthers;
2. reward collection or grooming fate;
3. conspecific pollen export or stigma deposition for the same visitor class.

### Route B — pollen-removal manipulation

Measure:

1. manipulated or quantified pollen removal;
2. response in pollen export, stigma deposition, fruit set, or seed set.

The historical observation of pollen extraction plus stigma contact remains biologically useful but is prospectively declared insufficient by itself.

## Why freeze this now

Without a prospective contract, the programme could lower the evidence threshold after seeing an attractive source or field observation.

The frozen rule prevents:

```text
interesting observation
-> redefine effective pollination/conflict
-> declare gate closed
```

The allowed direction is:

```text
freeze evidence route
-> collect/recover exact evidence
-> adjudicate against the frozen route
```

## Claim ceiling

This is a measurement protocol, not an empirical result. It does not close any current gate or imply that the missing biological process is absent.
