## Malvaceae closed independently

`Mollia lepidota` is now registered as a pre-2010 independent Malvaceae representative.

The evidence is species-specific and redundant:

1. Darwin's herbarium manuscript records `M. lepidota` as certainly having two stamen lengths within one flower.
2. Richard Spruce's 1876-77 notes explicitly include `M. lepidota` among four `Mollia` species with five longer outer stamen cohorts bearing green pollen and five shorter inner cohorts bearing yellow pollen.
3. Maguire's 1978 species treatment independently describes five outer stamen phalanges about 2 cm long and five inner phalanges about 1.5 cm long.
4. Current Kew taxonomy places `M. lepidota` in Malvaceae.

This closes the **independent family representative** requirement. It does **not** claim that Vallejo-Marín et al. (2010) used `M. lepidota` as the single Malvaceae row in inaccessible Table S1.
