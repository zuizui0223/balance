# BALANCE plant U3 matched-control audit v1

## Current state

The U3 heteranthery lane now has six source-resolved species cases across four families and two real screened PRIMARY control candidates.

Current controls:

1. `Solanum rostratum` -> `Solanum lycocarpum`
2. `Monochoria korsakowii` -> `Monochoria australasica`

Both remain `SCREENED`, not `ADJUDICATED`.

## Solanum pair

`Solanum rostratum` is a functional division-of-labour case with direct feeding-versus-pollinating anther evidence.

The control candidate `S. lycocarpum` was selected before BALANCE predictor extraction. Bohs et al. (2007) place the heterantherous section Androceras next to section Crinitum in the relevant Solanum phylogenetic context and describe Crinitum representatives as actinomorphic and isantherous. Independent floral-biology work documents bee/buzz-pollination in `S. lycocarpum`.

Because more than one section-Crinitum species can be considered at similar phylogenetic grain, `S. lycocarpum` is provisionally preferred under the registered SOURCE_QUALITY tie-break rather than by a BALANCE predictor contrast.

The pair is not yet adjudicated because the species-level nearest-control decision should be checked against a frozen phylogeny rather than inferred from sectional membership alone.

## Monochoria pair

`Monochoria korsakowii` is a direct pollen-fate conflict case in the U3 registry.

`M. australasica` is a congeneric candidate control. Primary comparative floral-development evidence reports no distinct pollinating anther and six yellow anthers, i.e. absence of the heterantherous feeding/pollinating anther split used to define the case architecture.

Modern plastid phylogenomics places `M. australasica` as sister to the rest of Monochoria and `M. korsakowii` as the second split. It is therefore congeneric but **not** the sister species of the case.

Comparative enantiostyly literature treats these systems as animal-pollinated, but the phylogenetic distance is why the match remains screened.

## Why screened controls are useful

A screened control candidate records a defensible pre-predictor selection path without pretending that matching is finished.

The registry is intentionally allowed to contain such provisional pairs. Only `ADJUDICATED` PRIMARY pairs count toward case-control closure.

Current readout therefore remains:

- 6 registered species cases;
- 2 screened PRIMARY control candidates;
- 0 adjudicated PRIMARY pairs;
- case-control layer open.

## Anti-bias rule

Control selection cannot use:

- measured conflict strength;
- BALANCE module-substrate score;
- timing/spatial conflict geometry;
- any later model residual;
- whether the control creates the desired coefficient.

Selection is limited to taxonomy/phylogeny, heteranthery absence, animal-pollination eligibility, source resolution, and the preregistered tie-break.

## Next control targets

The next useful work is not to add more heterantherous cases. It is to resolve non-heterantherous close relatives for:

- `Melastoma malabathricum`;
- `Monochoria vaginalis`;
- `Senna alata`;
- `Senna bicapsularis`.

The same control may not be treated as independent replication for multiple cases without explicit shared-control dependence.
