# BALANCE causal / direct-worldline falsifiers v1

BALANCE is not supported by conflict alone, by a sign-changing selection coefficient alone, or by positive dimensional release alone. The middle-world claim must survive the following falsifiers.

## F0 — no positive inherited conflict

If the context-matched SCH conflict interval does not lie above zero, Chapter 2 has no demonstrated conflict-active starting point.

## F1 — context or fitness-scale mismatch

If `W_S*` and `W_D*` come from different populations, seasons, life stages, or incomparable fitness scales, a direct middle-world ordering is not identified.

## F2 — architecture order is not shared-favouring

A direct BALANCE claim fails if

```text
Delta_W = W_D* - W_S*
```

is supported as positive. That is the differentiated/BITA side, not the BALANCE interior.

If the interval contains zero, classify the architecture order as unresolved rather than forcing either state.

## F3 — alternative worldline is not genuinely accessible/comparable

If the proposed second-axis condition changes unrelated environmental opportunities, plant condition, or outcome definition, `W_D*` is not a matched alternative worldline.

## F4 — direct middle-world geometry cannot be resolved

Do not report `rho`, `xi`, or `d_B` as identified BALANCE coordinates if the direct worldline ordering itself is unresolved.

## F5 — environmental crossing is unsupported

Do not infer a finite BALANCE width from two arbitrary contexts. A claimed environmental crossing requires ordered matched contexts and uncertainty that resolves the relevant side changes.

If no crossing is observed over a finite range, classify persistence over the observed path, not global no-crossing.

## F6 — re-entry contradicts the declared monotone model

Observed BALANCE -> DIFFERENTIATION -> BALANCE re-entry falsifies the monotone-path sufficient conditions for that path. Audit changes in conflict, recoverability, cost, world definition, and scale rather than averaging the re-entry away.

## F7 — hysteresis absent where a switching-cost claim is made

A static BALANCE result does not establish persistence hysteresis. If forward and reverse thresholds coincide within the resolution of a design intended to detect switching-cost hysteresis, the switching-cost mechanism is unsupported at that scale.

## Interpretation of a failure

A failed BALANCE test does not by itself prove BITA or PAYOFF. It can mean no conflict, unresolved worldline order, immediate differentiation, persistent shared advantage outside the sampled path, or an invalid comparator. Each alternative retains its own gate.
