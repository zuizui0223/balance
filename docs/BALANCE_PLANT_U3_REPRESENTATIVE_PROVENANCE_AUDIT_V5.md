# BALANCE U3 representative provenance audit v5

## Canonical family representative coverage

All 16 Figure-2 heteranthery-positive families have source-secure representatives. The three families whose exact 2010 Supporting Table S1 rows remain inaccessible are now represented entirely with independent **pre-2010** species-level evidence:

- Malvaceae — `Mollia lepidota`
- Bixaceae — `Amoreuxia wrightii`
- Scrophulariaceae — `Diascia anastrepta`

This replaces the previous post-review Scrophulariaceae representative `Verbascum phoeniceum`.

## Why Diascia anastrepta is stronger

Manning & Brothers (1986) directly describe marked heteranthery in `D. anastrepta`. The anticous anther pair is strongly reduced and produces little or no yellow pollen, whereas the posticous pair associated with the stigma produces copious grey-green pollen. Hilliard & Burtt had already published the species diagnosis in 1983. Current Kew taxonomy retains the species in Scrophulariaceae.

Thus the canonical representative no longer depends on evidence published after Vallejo-Marín et al. (2010).

## Exact Table S1 remains separate

The exact species printed in the inaccessible 2010 Table S1 remains archivally unresolved for Malvaceae, Bixaceae and Scrophulariaceae. Wiley download routes remain blocked and no usable archived copy has been recovered.

`Diascia anastrepta` is therefore an independently validated family representative, not a claimed transcription of the missing Table S1 row.

The archival candidate ledger deliberately remains OPEN for Scrophulariaceae exact-row identity.

## Claim ceiling

BALANCE may state that all 16 U3 families have representatives supported by review-body or independent pre-2010 evidence.

It may not state that `Mollia lepidota`, `Amoreuxia wrightii`, or `Diascia anastrepta` are the exact taxa printed in the inaccessible 2010 Supporting Table S1.
