**Figure 1. From conflict to the sandwiched BALANCE regime, and from literature recurrence to the remaining identification gap.**

The upper lane shows the theoretical sequence. A shared coordinate under opposing functional demands establishes active conflict (`L > 0`), but conflict alone does not identify BALANCE. BALANCE occupies the sandwiched regime in which conflict remains active while the optimized shared architecture still exceeds the accessible differentiated alternative (`rho = W_S* - W_D* > 0`). Contextual change can move the system toward a boundary at which relative worldline advantage changes sign and differentiation becomes favoured.

The lower lane shows the corresponding empirical recovery. The strict Q1B layer contains three independent effect-size-ready positive clusters and two design-matched negative controls. Recurrence is supported by recovery of the registered conflict pattern in the three independent positive systems, while the negative controls show that comparable designs do not inevitably produce it. Because positive Q1B admission used the focal conflict pattern, the three-cluster random-effects result is interpreted only as a conditional summary of magnitude and heterogeneity among admitted positives, not as an unbiased design-wide mean or an independent recurrence test. The broader source-adjudicated pattern ledger contains 17 independent clusters, including 9 middle-regime signatures, supporting recurrence of persistence geometry without estimating natural prevalence. Boundary and attribution cases are retained outside the strict Q1B numerator because they use distinct estimands or lack antagonist attribution. The final direct-worldline reserve remains empty: no current receipt jointly compares optimized shared and differentiated architectures on a common fitness scale and context, so `W_S*`, `W_D*`, `rho`, `Phi`, `xi`, and `d_B` remain unidentified by the literature synthesis.

The figure therefore distinguishes three claims: conflict recurrence, persistence-geometry recurrence, and direct BALANCE occupancy. Only the first two currently receive literature support; the third is reserved for final-stage focal validation and independent direct replications.
