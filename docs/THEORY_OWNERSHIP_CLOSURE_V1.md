# BALANCE theory ownership closure V1

## Decision

BALANCE is bounded by SCH on the conflict side and SLK on the architecture-value side. BITA is no longer the owner of the architecture-value boundary.

Canonical programme geometry:

```text
SCH
L = identified shared-coordinate conflict load
        |
        v
BALANCE
L > 0 and Phi < 0
        |
        v
SLK
Phi = R-K; under the registered quadratic bridge R=sL
Phi > 0 -> global architecture advantage only
        |
        v
accessibility -> invasion -> fixation -> occupancy
```

BITA runs orthogonally to this value hierarchy:

```text
measured A x D interaction
-> identified set
-> partial identification
-> selective consumer interventions
-> separability diagnostic
-> independently supported remaining channel
-> mechanism allocation
```

## Canonical ownership

- SCH owns identification of functional conflict and the admissibility of `L`.
- BALANCE owns the geometry, depth, reserve, topology and persistence of the region `L>0, Phi<0`, plus direct worldline comparison `Delta_W=W_D*-W_S*`.
- SLK owns `R`, the quadratic corollary `R=sL`, `K`, `Phi=R-K`, architecture-value crossing, accessibility, invasion, fixation and occupancy.
- BITA owns the statement `trait interaction != mechanism` and the identification machinery for channel allocation.

## Boundary terminology

From this closure onward:

```text
SCH-facing boundary = L=0
SLK-facing boundary = Phi=0
```

Legacy occurrences of `BITA-facing boundary` refer historically to the same architecture-value surface but are deprecated terminology after the SLK split.

## General versus model-specific statements

The general architecture margin is

```text
Phi = R-K.
```

The relation

```text
R=sL
```

is a registered bridge/corollary, not a universal identity for arbitrary landscapes. Therefore BALANCE results that use `s` are conditional on that bridge. Direct worldline results based on

```text
Delta_W = W_D* - W_S*
```

do not require `R=sL`.

## Direct/decomposed reconciliation

BALANCE may identify the middle world directly from

```text
L>0 and Delta_W<0.
```

Under a valid shared bridge, the decomposed representation predicts

```text
Delta_W = Phi = R-K,
```

and under the quadratic bridge

```text
Delta_W = sL-K.
```

A non-zero bridge residual is retained as mismatch; it is not assigned automatically to BITA mechanism terms, SLK architecture cost, or an omitted biological channel.

## Closed claim boundaries

BALANCE must not claim that:

- `Phi>0` means differentiation evolves;
- `R=sL` is universal;
- a bridge residual identifies a biological mechanism;
- BITA owns the architecture-value threshold;
- direct worldline ordering identifies ecological channel allocation.

BITA mechanism decomposition and SLK architecture-value decomposition are different objects and must not be algebraically conflated merely because both use fitness differences.

## Closure status

With this ownership contract, the theoretical handoff is:

```text
SCH: identify conflict
BALANCE: classify/localize persistent compromise
SLK: value and realization hierarchy
BITA: mechanism identification for trait interactions
```

No additional focal experimental design is required to close this theoretical ownership structure.
