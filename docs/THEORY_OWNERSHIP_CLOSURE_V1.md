# BALANCE theory ownership closure V1

## Decision

BALANCE is bounded by SCH on the conflict side and SLK on the architecture-value side. BITA is not the owner of the architecture-value boundary.

```text
SCH
L = identified shared-coordinate conflict load
        |
        v
BALANCE
L > 0 and Phi < 0
        |
        v
SLK
Phi = R-K; under the registered quadratic bridge R=sL
Phi > 0 -> global architecture advantage only
        |
        v
accessibility -> invasion -> fixation -> occupancy
```

BITA runs orthogonally:

```text
measured A x D interaction
-> identified set
-> partial identification
-> selective interventions
-> separability diagnostic
-> independently supported remaining channel
-> mechanism allocation
```

## Canonical ownership

- SCH owns identification of functional conflict and admissibility of `L`.
- BALANCE owns geometry, depth, reserve, topology and persistence of `L>0, Phi<0`, plus direct worldline comparison `Delta_W=W_D*-W_S*`.
- SLK owns `R`, `K`, `Phi=R-K`, the registered quadratic corollary `R=sL`, architecture-value crossing, accessibility, invasion, fixation and occupancy.
- BITA owns `trait interaction != mechanism` and identification of ecological channel allocation.

## Boundary terminology

```text
SCH-facing boundary = L=0
SLK-facing boundary = Phi=0
```

Legacy occurrences of `BITA-facing boundary` refer historically to the same architecture-value surface but are deprecated after the SLK split.

## Legacy API compatibility

Existing public code may still expose identifiers such as `bita_differentiation_favoured`, `bita_boundary_distance`, `finite_bita_boundary`, `bita_limited_width`, `BITA_DIFFERENTIATION_WORLD`, or `BALANCE_BITA_INTERFACE`. These are retained temporarily as **backward-compatible API aliases only**. They do not assign scientific ownership of `Phi=0` to BITA. New prose and new APIs should prefer `slk_*` or neutral `architecture_*` terminology. Do not bulk-rename legacy fields without downstream migration and regression tests.

## General versus model-specific statements

The general architecture margin is

```text
Phi = R-K.
```

The relation

```text
R=sL
```

is a registered quadratic bridge/corollary, not a universal identity. BALANCE results using `s` are conditional on that bridge. Direct worldline results based on `Delta_W=W_D*-W_S*` do not require `R=sL`.

## Direct/decomposed reconciliation

BALANCE may be identified directly from `L>0` and `Delta_W<0`. Under a valid shared bridge,

```text
Delta_W = Phi = R-K,
```

and under the quadratic bridge `Delta_W=sL-K`. A non-zero bridge residual is retained as mismatch; it is not assigned automatically to BITA mechanism terms, SLK architecture cost, or a named biological channel.

## Closed claim boundaries

BALANCE must not claim that `Phi>0` means differentiation evolves, that `R=sL` is universal, that a bridge residual identifies a biological mechanism, that BITA owns the architecture-value threshold, or that direct worldline ordering identifies ecological channel allocation.

BITA mechanism decomposition and SLK architecture-value decomposition are different objects and must not be algebraically conflated merely because both use fitness differences.

## Closure status

```text
SCH: identify conflict
BALANCE: classify/localize persistent compromise
SLK: value and realization hierarchy
BITA: mechanism identification for trait interactions
```

No additional focal experiment or new theorem is required to close this theoretical ownership structure. Remaining work is editorial synchronization, empirical testing, or optional extension beyond the registered model classes.
