## The problem changes after U3

The original coarse question was:

> does pollen-use conflict predict heteranthery?

The frozen U3 matched lane shows why that is too coarse.

Three nonheterantherous controls already retain direct pollen-reward versus gamete-transfer
conflict, and two of those controls occupy two different routing states in two dependence
blocks. The matched Senna pair also supplies the reverse counterexample: the same
within-flower routing state occurs with and without heteranthery morphology.

Therefore three empirical objects must remain separate:

1. functional conflict;
2. routing architecture;
3. visible morphological implementation.

The explanatory target becomes conditional:

> given a demonstrated conflict, what predicts where the lineage routes it?

## Discovery lane: U3

U3 is retained as a hypothesis-generating and identification-diagnostic lane.

It establishes narrow logical results:

- binary conflict presence is not sufficient to identify heteranthery;
- visible heteranthery is not necessary for within-flower functional division;
- nonheteranthery does not imply shared/global integration;
- at least two routing states are already identified among positive nonheterantherous
  controls.

Those results motivate the routing question and make module opportunity biologically
plausible as a routing predictor.

They do **not** license using U3 as confirmatory response data.

## Why this separation matters

Using U3 both to discover a module-routing pattern and then again as V4 response evidence
would blur hypothesis generation and testing.

The programme instead uses:

```text
U3 matched counterexamples
-> define the conditional-routing problem
-> motivate candidate mechanism

U2 + U6 outcome-blind universes
-> independent confirmatory architecture coding
-> frozen V4 test
```

This makes a null or reversed V4 result informative rather than something to be repaired
using the discovery lane.

## Falsification logic

If H_M is null or reversed, the resolved U3 control pattern remains a discovery
observation; it is not promoted to a general module-routing law.

If H_T fails the cross-universe decision rule, timing is not described as a general routing
predictor across the two conflict universes.

If `S. covesii` resolves to a route different from its frozen U3 prediction, that
contradiction is retained and does not alter the V4 coding or model.

## Scientific role

The combined programme therefore makes a stronger and less circular claim than
“heteranthery resolves pollen conflict.”

The target is:

> conflict can persist under multiple visible morphologies and multiple organizational
> routing solutions; the confirmatory question is which pre-existing ecological geometry
> predicts those routing solutions.

Whether module opportunity or temporal exposure actually predicts routing remains an
unopened empirical question until independent U2/U6 coding returns.

## Claim ceiling

This document fixes the discovery/confirmation boundary and the inferential sequence.
It does not establish a V4 effect, a cross-universe generality result, a causal transition,
or a publication decision.
