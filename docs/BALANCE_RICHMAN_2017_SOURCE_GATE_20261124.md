# Richman et al. 2017: original-study source gate
Article DOI 10.1111/1365-2745.12657; Dryad DOI 10.5061/dryad.tf71f. Same published experiment, not an independent new study. Original whole-plant manipulation, 2013, initially 40 plants in each of three haphazardly assigned groups: control, once-primary, primary plus repeated secondary robbing. The design does not have a secondary-only arm; no factorial interaction or synergy claim. Primary+secondary versus primary also changes robbery frequency and collar exposure. Stigma collection and subsequent fruit measurements must be linked at actual plant ID, not assumed same flowers.
