# T — Theory

## T1. Sandwiched-domain definition



Status: `DEFINED_AND_IMPLEMENTED`.

## T2. Interior geometry and deepest point

Quantify where a system lies inside the middle world using direct same-scale margins:

```text
rho   = W_S* - W_D* = -Delta_W
xi    = L/(L+rho)
d_B   = min(L,rho)
```

A direct theorem removes the need for the quadratic decomposition. Along any connected environmental path where `L(e)` increases continuously and `rho(e)` decreases continuously, with the two margins straddling one another, there is one and only one deepest BALANCE point:

```text
L(e_deep) = rho(e_deep)
xi(e_deep) = 1/2.
```

The two-sided depth `d_B` increases before this point and decreases after it. `xi` is monotone along the path. Common positive rescaling of the fitness margin leaves `xi` and `e_deep` unchanged.

The fixed-`s,K` quadratic ridge

```text
L_deep = K/(1+s)
```

is a special case.

Status: `GENERAL_DEEPEST_MIDDLE_WORLD_THEOREM_PROVED_AND_IMPLEMENTED`.

## T3. Domain topology and continuous-domain certification

If along environment `e`:

```text
L(e) nondecreasing
s(e) nondecreasing
K(e) nonincreasing
```

then the architecture margin is nondecreasing and BALANCE -> DIFFERENTIATION -> BALANCE re-entry is impossible on that path.

The separate domain-existence classifier distinguishes no positive-width BALANCE, finite BALANCE, and persistence over the observed path.

The current theory also supplies multi-alternative envelopes, accessibility-scope bounds, environmental/metric depth, affine and concave-domain certificates, Helly obstruction certificates, threat-switch/stability results, and finite-sample Lipschitz covering / lower-envelope / local-ball certificates. These results refine what a middle regime would look like across environmental space; they do not themselves establish natural occupancy.

Status: `ADVANCED_DOMAIN_GEOMETRY_AND_CERTIFICATES_IMPLEMENTED`.

## T4. Persistence / hysteresis

With switching costs and a finite context horizon, derive a no-switch band and forward/reverse threshold separation. This is a state-persistence result distinct from PAYOFF frequency-dependent coordination.

Status: `SWITCHING_COST_THEORY_IMPLEMENTED`.

### Theory claim ceiling

The theory defines a possible and measurable middle regime. It does not establish that a natural system actually occupies it, nor that any observed sign reversal is a direct worldline crossing.
