# BALANCE structural-worldline primary-source gate v1

**Status: source eligibility only / zero matched direct structural-worldline receipts / no new biological dataset.**

This is a deliberately strict follow-on to the merged [Pedicularis nested-comparator correction PR #225](https://github.com/zuizui0223/balance/pull/225). The previous water-on/off comparison is a **functional-state** manipulation; a negative fixed-state gap cannot establish a structurally differentiated architecture with a net cost. Neither a differential organ function nor the absence of a statistically detectable investment cost is enough to identify the structural BALANCE middle world.

## Scientific question

**When there is a genuine quantified functional conflict, is a specifically registered, accessible alternative structural architecture nevertheless less fit than the shared architecture *in the same environment*?**

A source can become eligible for the direct structural test only when it supplies, within a matched biological context:

1. Independently identified SCH functional conflict budget `L>0` **on a commensurable fitness scale**;
2. A measured shared architecture and a measured, genuinely different structural alternative, **not two functional switch states of the same organ**;
3. A registered non-nested architecture comparison, or a nested comparison with independently measured/justified net architecture cost **already included in the alternative's fitness**;
4. Matched whole-system fitness (reproductive success or well-defined growth/competition) for both architectures, rather than an upstream proxy such as pollen deposition, fluorescence intensity or flower counts;
5. Prospectively fixed environmental context, architecture-accessibility scope, ecological functional demands, optimization/fitness semantics and comparison direction;
6. Optimized shared and alternative net worldlines with **joint uncertainty**, source row lineage, and legitimate biological replication.

The bounded direct classification is *downstream* of these source gates. The source gate never estimates fitness or promotes biological occupancy. If a retrospective study lacked a prospectively frozen comparator, its descriptive evidence can seed a new preregistered experiment but cannot be relabelled confirmatory.

### Mathematical comparator rule

For a fixed objective without architecture cost, expanding from a shared feasible set `X_S` to `X_S ∪ X_D` cannot lower the optimum:

`max_{u in X_S ∪ X_D} W(u) >= max_{u in X_S} W(u)`.

This is a set-inclusion identity, not biological evidence. A negative net structural gap is possible only for a genuinely distinct constrained architecture, or with an independently justified architecture-specific cost/constraint incorporated into net fitness. Comparing current functional water-on vs water-off at *Pedicularis* does neither.

The `Dalechampia`, `Solanum` and coGFP records overlap existing BALANCE pattern/other source programmes. This audit is a **stricter rereading of existing and adjacent sources**, not 5 additional independent clusters, not a direct-worldline meta-analysis, and not an estimate of natural prevalence. Existing population, taxon and study independence ceilings are retained.

## Concrete next direct-test design — two possible lanes

### Lane A: plant architecture (highest ecological face validity; highest experimental difficulty)

Candidate: a morphology-controlled, within-lineage *Solanum* heteranthery system with matched viable isomorphic/homomorphic S and differentiated-anther D structures. Do **not** use a simple anther blockage/emasculation as a substitute for a structural state; such manipulations simultaneously change pollen supply, bee behaviour and structural integrity.

Required before trial: identify a naturally segregating, near-isogenic, genetically complemented, or independently engineered S/D pair. Verify anther length/position and developmental identity, pollen production, bee manipulation, and plant condition. Randomize *independent plants* across defined pollinator and pollen-removal regimes. Measure the same net fitness quantity for each architecture, ideally both male siring and female seed fitness; predeclare how male and female components combine rather than post-hoc scalarizing them. If they cannot be combined on a valid common scale, **retain a vector/Pareto comparison** and report structural BALANCE as unidentified.

**Critical blocker:** no qualifying S/D pair or matched net fitness has been established from the audited 2009 paper. No biological power estimate is justified now.

### Lane B: genetic structural architecture (closer to controllable constructs, not a plant replication)

Candidate: an explicitly **new** engineered isogenic *Salmonella* HisA-system comparison of one bifunctional enzymatic solution S versus a matched duplicated/partitioned specialist construction D, tested under one registered joint histidine+tryptophan ecological requirement.

Keep genomic locus, marker, promoter regime, copy dosage, compensatory mutations and nutrient history documented. Measure competitive relative growth or log fitness per generation in matched contexts, not just enzymatic activity or optical-density improvement. Use a sufficiently dense shared functional-coordinate allelic series and independent selective treatments to identify `L` without reusing D as the antagonist proxy. Cross independent genetic constructs/replicates with nutritional regimes and obtain joint uncertainty. A stable D construct, its biological accessibility, the net growth cost of extra expression/dosage, and its common fitness scale are all **necessary data to create**, not features established by Näsvall & Abdalaal (2026).

If no biologically meaningful source of common selection pressure or no feasible structural D exists, the project remains an *experimental proposal*, not a structural BALANCE receipt. This is not a claim that genetic duplication causes floral morphology or plant macroevolution.

## Research conclusion

The strongest present contribution is a **specific, refutable measurement requirement**: structural differentiation can only be said to lose to persistent integration after matched net architecture performance is measured and nested-set/cost, source identity and ecological confounding are ruled out. Floral morphology alone, functional pollen routing, loss of water defence, fluorescent-protein copy number, or null costs in maternal seed output cannot substitute for this comparison.

Source evidence supports several *ingredients* but **zero combined direct structural BALANCE receipts**. The next scientific advance requires a genuinely new matched experiment, not more interpolation of the current source ledger.
