# One flower species, multiple functional conflicts: an ecological multiplex

**Status:** source-reported original-study synthesis of TWO previously defined, outcome-enriched literature frames. It is not a new observational sample, confirmatory cross-species association, meta-analysis, independent coder adjudication, or direct structural BALANCE test.

## What is new?

**A species is not a single shared-versus-specialized architecture state.** One species can carry opposing ecological demands on different organs at the same time, and the degree of functional separation must be indexed to the function pair, target organ and ecological exposure regime.

This is source-testable in two independent original studies of **Polemonium viscosum**, with a complementary exposure-dependence example in **Narcissus assoanus**. Full source-registered U2 sexual-interference screening (22 species) was joined with the existing BALANCE plant comparative pilot (29 records / 28 dependency groups). ## Direct positive ecological details on their own outcome scales

### Polemonium — coexistence of two competing functional edges

Galen, Gregory & Galloway (1989) documented self-pollen interference in the self-incompatible alpine skypilot. When self pollen was applied 24 h before cross pollen in the first experimental year, compatible pollen germination fell **32%** and seed set **40%**. In the following year, self pollen again inhibited compatible pollen germination, yet a corresponding seed-set reduction was not detected under the registered experimental conditions. This supports functional interference potential, with year- and stage-dependent conversion to seed costs.

Primary: https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1537-2197.1989.tb15152.x (American Journal of Botany).

Galen & Cuba (2001) studied a **different functional opposition** within the same named species: long, broad corollas attract bumblebee pollinators yet incur vulnerability to flower-visiting, style-severing ants. Corolla length and flare had a reported **genetic correlation 0.70**. Experimentally imposed narrower tubular flowers reduced ant entry while also reducing received pollen and seed production in a bumblebee-pollinated context.

Primary: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.0014-3820.2001.tb01313.x (Evolution).

The original studies demonstrate two different ecological conflicts, and a source-coded contrast between pollen-stigma *temporal/spatial avoidance* and corolla *integrated-function compromise*. They do **not** reveal a switch of one structure between states or measure the net fitness of a separately differentiated corolla. Do not pool the 1989 percentage seed cost, 2001 ant/bee treatment and genetic correlation on any one numerical fitness axis.

### Narcissus — experimental potential need not equal realized natural cost

Cesaro et al. (2004) found that self pollen simultaneously applied with cross pollen reduced seed set by approximately **20%**, whereas self pollen given **24 h before cross pollen** reduced it approximately **60%** under controlled experiments. But the same publication **did not detect male or female fitness costs attributable to self-interference under natural field conditions**.

Distinct morphology-linked candidate avoidance mechanisms were observed in the polymorphism:
- Long-styled morph: delayed early stigma receptivity, **protandry / temporal separation**.
- Short-styled morph: pronounced anther–stigma **herkogamy / spatial separation**.

Primary: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1420-9101.2004.00767.x (Journal of Evolutionary Biology).

This illustrates an ecological distinction between **potential interference under forced exposure** and **realized interference under the ordinary pollination schedule**. It does not prove zero hidden conflict in nature or that sexual conflict alone caused the historical style polymorphism. The earlier 2000 sources screened as NO_DEMONSTRATED_CONFLICT because they did not directly identify the same interference cost; this should not be interpreted as a contradictory controlled negative experiment.

### Solanum — pollen food and gamete export differ from sexual self-interference

Todd (1882) described heteranthery morphology, but that historical source does not experimentally identify same-flower male–female self-pollen interference. Vallejo-Marín et al. (2009) directly showed that bees preferentially manipulate **feeding anthers**, whereas **pollinating anthers export more pollen** in flight cages: a different, pollen reward versus male gamete function.

Primary: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1420-9101.2009.01693.x.

The historical U2 source's NO_DEMONSTRATED label is limited to that source and functional question, not a statement that the species has no reproductive conflict.

### Ipomopsis — uncertainty about another pair is not a measured negative

Waser & Price (1991) provide a positive original source experiment for interference between incompatible self pollen and compatible outcross reproduction.

Primary: https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1537-2197.1991.tb14511.x.

The separate Irwin (2006) nectar-robber/pollinator source remains UNRESOLVED for strict shared-coordinate opposed optima under the existing source codebook (https://doi.org/10.1086/499377). Do not turn lack of this specific test into a negative biological result.

## A real upstream hypothesis for future macroecology

**H_multiplex:** The expression and architecture of a functional conflict depend on **the function pair, shared organ coordinate and ecological exposure conditions**, not on a binary property assigned to the entire species.

A properly designed future database should record one row per biologically tested *taxon × function pair × structure × environment/experiment* with species and phylogeny as higher-level grouping factors. Distinguish:

1. **Conflict detection:** positive experimentally identified opposing demands; untested; aligned/null for an adequately designed direct test.
2. **Exposure:** potential cost under forced interference vs naturally realized cost under normal reproductive activity, with season/year and microenvironment.
3. **Resolution:** shared trait compromise, temporal gate, spatial gate, within-module or among-module functional routing, genuinely distinct structural modules, or unresolved.
4. **Fitness:** male pollen export/outcross siring and female mature viable seeds on the correct scales; no arbitrary conversion of pollen germination % into whole-plant fitness.

Hypothesis tests should compare *within-species* function-pair levels after freezing an **outcome-blind species-first sampling frame**, independently extracting at least two mechanistically distinct tested function pairs per species. Use a species/phylogenetic hierarchy and account for observation and evidence-effort bias. The current four matched cases are **selected examples**, not an unbiased incidence of multiplex conflicts or an ecological odds ratio.

**A first falsifiable prospective experiment:** manipulate self-pollen precedence versus natural reproductive timing and ant exposure versus corolla presentation *in the same season and species* of Polemonium viscosum, with explicitly recorded floral organs, bee pollen receipt and mature seed outcomes. The two perturbation classes would need handling, pollen donor and plant-level randomization controls; the direction of the interaction is unknown. A positive interaction would demonstrate context-dependent coupling of two conflicts but **not** the relative net fitness of two distinct structural architectures required for strict BALANCE.
