# BALANCE manuscript spine v1

## Working title

**Why conflict persists: a theory and evidence synthesis of the sandwiched regime between integration and differentiation**

Alternative shorter title:

**Persistence under conflict: the sandwiched regime between integration and differentiation**

## One-sentence paper claim

When one biological coordinate faces incompatible functional demands, conflict does not imply immediate differentiation: a finite middle regime can persist in which the shared architecture remains favoured, and the literature contains recurrent conflict-compatible and persistence signatures while still lacking the matched worldline comparisons required for direct BALANCE occupancy.

## Abstract draft

Biological traits often serve multiple functions whose preferred states do not coincide, yet conflict does not always lead to functional differentiation. We develop a theory of this intermediate outcome. A shared architecture can remain favoured inside a finite **sandwiched regime** when functional conflict is active but the recoverable benefit of differentiation does not exceed the costs, constraints, or lost coordination associated with splitting functions. The theory separates three objects: conflict itself, persistence of an integrated state under conflict, and the boundary at which a differentiated alternative becomes favoured. We then ask whether the corresponding signatures recur in existing biological systems before designing a focal direct test. A source-adjudicated literature synthesis recovered 17 independent clusters, including nine middle-regime signatures. A stricter quantitative lane identified three independent factorial systems that met a preregistered positive Q1B admission rule for opposing agent-mediated selection on a shared coordinate. Because positive admission depended on the observed conflict pattern, the random-effects summary of these three systems is interpreted only as a conditional description of effect magnitude and heterogeneity among admitted positives, not as an unbiased estimate of a general mean effect or as an independent test of recurrence. All modified Knapp–Hartung 95% intervals included zero at `k = 3`, with moderate heterogeneity in two contrasts. Recurrence is instead supported by recovery of the registered pattern in three independent positive systems, while two design-matched negative controls, two estimand boundaries, and one antagonist-attribution failure show that the conflict pattern is conditional rather than inevitable and that apparently similar cases can belong to different inferential objects. No current literature receipt directly compares optimized shared and differentiated architectures on a common fitness scale and context. The synthesis therefore supports recurrence of conflict-compatible and persistence geometry, but not direct BALANCE occupancy. It also identifies the final empirical target: matched shared-versus-differentiated worldlines measured only after the literature-defined pattern and identification gaps are established.

## Paper architecture

### 1. Introduction — the missing middle between conflict and differentiation

Start from the biological problem rather than from one focal system:

1. multifunctional traits can face incompatible demands;
2. conflict alone does not imply that functions split into separate structures;
3. differentiation has benefits but also costs, constraints, or coordination losses;
4. therefore the key missing question is **when conflict persists without splitting**;
5. define BALANCE as the middle / sandwiched regime between shared-coordinate conflict and differentiation.

End the Introduction with two linked aims:

- **theory:** establish the existence, geometry, and boundaries of the sandwiched regime;
- **reality-pattern recovery:** test whether its prerequisite, persistence signatures, negative cases, and transition boundaries recur in the literature before any final focal experiment.

The Introduction must not promise direct empirical estimation of `rho`, `Phi`, `xi`, or `d_B` from the meta-synthesis.

### 2. Theory — a finite conflict-active persistence regime

Core theoretical sequence:

```text
conflict active
L > 0
    ↓
shared and differentiated worldlines are both biologically admissible
    ↓
rho = W_S* - W_D*
    ↓
rho > 0  -> shared architecture persists under conflict
rho = 0  -> boundary
rho < 0  -> differentiated alternative favoured
```

Theory subsections should draw from the established repository modules and proofs rather than re-derive ad hoc empirical formulas:

- existence of the domain;
- geometry / depth of the persistence region;
- environmental paths and boundary crossing;
- multi-alternative and accessibility extensions;
- hysteresis/path-dependence extension where supported;
- identification limits separating observable pattern signatures from direct worldline quantities.

### 3. Empirical synthesis Methods — recover signatures before direct validation

The empirical workflow is deliberately ordered after the theory:

1. translate each theoretical object into observable literature signatures;
2. adjudicate independent biological clusters rather than count papers, sites, traits, or years as independent replication;
3. retain positive, negative, boundary, and attribution-failure cases;
4. pool only compatible effect-size lanes with identified uncertainty;
5. preserve direct-worldline quantities as missing when existing studies do not measure them;
6. reserve new focal empirical design for the final unresolved identification target.

#### 3.1 Broad pattern ledger

Primary outputs:

- conflict without splitting;
- persistent integration with an available alternative;
- sandwiched transition mosaics;
- boundary crossing;
- direct differentiation boundaries;
- unresolved cases.

This ledger estimates recurrence within a screened source-adjudicated universe, **not natural prevalence**.

#### 3.2 Strict Q1B quantitative lane

Design compatibility requires:

- independent biological cluster;
- identified pollinator and antagonist manipulation/context;
- same biologically defined trait coordinate;
- common reproductive-fitness interpretation;
- compatible factorial context-specific contrast vector;
- identified full joint uncertainty from reported sufficient statistics or raw-data resampling.

Positive admission additionally requires the frozen same-coordinate conflict pattern. Current positive-admitted systems are:

```text
Fragaria vesca
Impatiens capensis
Gymnadenia conopsea 2015
```

Design-matched negatives remain outside the positive numerator. Consequently, the random-effects summary of the three positives is a **conditional positive-case summary**, not an unbiased design-wide meta-analytic mean and not an independent test of recurrence.

#### 3.3 Boundary and attribution lanes

Keep separate:

- sequential-filter conflict;
- discrete-morph conflict;
- design-matched null/negative controls;
- statistical opposition without identified antagonist attribution.

#### 3.4 Quantitative model

First permitted conditional positive-case summary:

- componentwise DerSimonian–Laird random effects;
- modified Knapp–Hartung intervals;
- `k = 3` independent positive-admitted clusters;
- full within-study covariance retained;
- no attempt to estimate an underidentified 4 × 4 between-study covariance at `k = 3`.

Moderator analysis is preregistered but prohibited at `k = 3–4`. First exploratory meta-regression requires `k >= 5` and at least two independent clusters in each represented moderator level.

### 6. Final empirical test — only after the synthesis-defined gap

The focal empirical design belongs at the end of the programme, not at the front of the paper's justification.

Its job is narrowly defined by the failed literature identification:

```text
same context
same fitness scale
optimized shared architecture W_S*
registered accessible differentiated alternative W_D*
joint uncertainty
```

Only this layer can directly estimate strict BALANCE occupancy and derived worldline quantities. Pedicularis or another focal system is therefore a **final direct-validation candidate**, not the primary evidence on which the general theory depends.

## Claim ladder

### Established theoretically

- a finite conflict-active persistence / sandwiched regime can exist;
- the shared-to-differentiated boundary is a relative-worldline condition;
- persistence geometry can be extended to context paths, accessibility, and history under stated assumptions.

### Supported empirically by literature synthesis

- the registered same-coordinate conflict pattern recurs in three independent compatible quantitative systems;
- comparable designs can also fail to show that conflict;
- conflict-like patterns can live on different estimands or fail biological attribution;
- middle-regime persistence signatures recur across independent systems and domains;
- effect magnitude and heterogeneity can be described conditionally within the current positive-admitted Q1B set.

### Not yet directly identified

- a design-wide/population-average Q1B mean effect;
- `W_S*` and `W_D*` on matched natural-system worldlines;
- `rho`, `Phi`, `xi`, `d_B` in a focal natural system;
- natural prevalence of BALANCE;
- architecture-specific hysteresis with matched forward/reverse thresholds.

## Current stopping rule before focal experiment

Do not move the paper's empirical main line to a new focal experiment while literature-synthesis work can still improve:

- source adjudication;
- compatible effect extraction;
- negative-control specificity;
- boundary classification;
- preregistered moderator coverage.

Move to the final focal worldline test only for quantities that remain structurally unidentified after those routes are exhausted.
