# BALANCE technical certification module v1

## Purpose

BALANCE is retained as a citable technical module, not as an active standalone paper. Its job is to certify and quantify the **middle world** in which a multifunctional shared trait remains genuinely conflicted but accessible differentiation is not yet fitter.

The module therefore sits between two stronger claims:

```text
SCH: identify genuine shared-coordinate conflict and export L
BALANCE: certify L > 0 while differentiation remains disadvantageous
SLK: transport architecture value onward to accessibility, invasion, fixation and occupancy
```

BALANCE does not own downstream evolutionary outcome claims.

## Core certificate

When the registered decomposition is available, the middle world is

```text
L > 0
Phi < 0.
```

For direct optimized shared-versus-differentiated worldlines, define

```text
Delta_W = W_D* - W_S*.
```

Then the direct certificate is

```text
L > 0
Delta_W < 0.
```

The direct reserve against differentiation is

```text
rho = W_S* - W_D* = -Delta_W > 0.
```

Two derived diagnostics locate and quantify the certified state:

```text
xi  = L/(L+rho)
d_B = min(L,rho).
```

Inside BALANCE, `0 < xi < 1`. The limit `xi -> 0` approaches the SCH-facing no-conflict boundary, whereas `xi -> 1` approaches the SLK-facing architecture-value boundary. The depth `d_B` is the smaller margin to either boundary.

## What the module retains

The DOI surface retains the repository's machinery for:

- direct optimized-worldline identification of the middle world;
- decomposed and direct certificate concordance;
- position `xi`, reserve `rho` and two-sided depth `d_B`;
- environmental width, connectivity and topology diagnostics;
- boundary crossing and no-reentry sufficient conditions;
- switching-cost hysteresis and persistence diagnostics;
- source-adjudicated reality-pattern synthesis;
- reproducible figure/readout contracts and fail-closed validation.

These are diagnostic and certification results. They are not themselves evidence that differentiated architecture invades, fixes, or dominates long-run occupancy.

## Empirical claim ceiling

The current evidence layer is a source-adjudicated pattern synthesis, not a direct matched worldline demonstration.

The frozen reality-pattern readout contains 17 independent biological clusters, including 9 middle-regime signature clusters. It is used as **pattern recovery**, not as an estimate of natural prevalence. The canonical manuscript manifest records

```text
direct_worldline_status = 0_direct_matched_receipts.
```

Accordingly, the module must not promote:

- a general prevalence of the BALANCE state in nature;
- a pooled direct estimate of `Phi`, `rho`, `xi`, or `d_B` across unmatched systems;
- direct BALANCE occupancy from literature-pattern recurrence;
- downstream accessibility, invasion, fixation or occupancy conclusions.

## Relation to SLK

SLK owns the broader transport spine

```text
L -> R -> Phi -> accessibility -> invasion -> fixation -> occupancy.
```

BALANCE only supplies the middle-world classification and diagnostics underneath that spine. In particular,

```text
L > 0  does not imply Phi > 0,
```

and a certified BALANCE state explicitly occupies the opposite sign regime.

## Reactivation rule

A standalone BALANCE paper should be reconsidered only if at least one of the following becomes true:

1. direct empirical worldlines recover a nontrivial middle-world geometry that is itself a biological result;
2. a theorem family emerges that is independent of SLK state classification and cannot be reduced to diagnostic elaboration;
3. an empirical hysteresis or persistence result establishes a distinct scientific question.

Until then:

```text
STATUS = DOI_MODULE / DORMANT_PAPER_BRANCH
ACTIVE_PUBLICATION_QUEUE = false
```
